## What exists
- `Power4_Athletic_Department_Benchmarking.xlsx`: a finished, formula-driven analyst workbook benchmarking 68 athletic departments: the 67 Power 4 schools (ACC 17, Big Ten 18, Big 12 16, SEC 16) and Notre Dame as a football independent. 53 schools have public NCAA MFRS financial data. The other 15 (the 14 private universities including Notre Dame, and Pittsburgh) have no public MFRS, so their financials are the federal EADA 2024-25 report (fiscal 2025): total revenue, total expenses, football revenue, men's basketball revenue and unallocated revenue only, with every other financial line shown as "--". EADA and MFRS are not comparable and are never averaged together in the scoped summary rows.
- Tabs (names carry no number prefix): `Read Me`; `All Schools` (all formulas linking to the conference and Independent tabs, then the same summary rows and Data breakdown table as a conference tab, for all schools only; no per-conference rows); `ACC`, `Big Ten`, `Big 12`, `SEC`, `Independent` (Notre Dame only) (the only tabs with hardcoded values, one row per school, then summary rows and a Data breakdown table); `Source Log` (one row per sourced value: school, conference, metric, value, unit, fiscal year, source type, source name, URL, date accessed, note); `School Lookup` (yellow input cell with data validation, INDEX/MATCH list). There are no per-school tabs.
- Summary rows: Average, Median and Count (schools with a value), each across a grid of fiscal year (FY2025, FY2024, all years) by data source (MFRS, EADA, all sources), leaving out combinations with no schools: FY2025 MFRS, FY2025 EADA, FY2025 all sources, FY2024 MFRS, FY2024 all sources, all years MFRS, all years EADA, and overall. Scoped rows cover the financial columns only and filter on the Financial data type and Fiscal year columns, whose cells in the summary row hold the criteria. The Data breakdown table counts schools by data type and fiscal year, and by financial source basis.
- Metric columns, in order: Identity (School, Conference, Public or Private, State); Financial (Fiscal year, Financial source basis, Financial data type [formula: MFRS or EADA], Total operating revenue, Football revenue, Men's basketball revenue, Other sports revenue [formula], Non-program-specific revenue, Total operating expenses, Operating surplus [formula], Media rights, Ticket sales, Contributions, NCAA and conference distributions, Direct institutional support, Indirect institutional support, Student fees, Total subsidy [formula = direct + student fees + indirect], Subsidy as % of revenue [formula], Total coaching salaries and benefits, Total athletics-related debt, Annual debt service); Coaching (FB and MBB head coach name, annual guaranteed compensation, contract end year, pay source type); Venues (stadium name, capacity, year opened, last renovation; arena name, capacity, year opened); Attendance (football from the D1.ticker FBS Attendance Trends pages: average home attendance 2023, 2024, 2025, year-over-year change 2025, 5-year average, change vs 5-year average, % of capacity 2025, all as reported; men's basketball from the NCAA Men's Basketball Attendance Records PDF, 2025-26: home games, total home attendance, average home attendance, % of capacity [formula = average / arena capacity], and four top-30-only columns from the NCAA leader tables: all games played, all-games attendance, 2024-25 average and change in average, with no average or median); Stadium naming rights and Arena naming rights (partner, industry, deal type, start year, term, annual value, total value, expires [formula]); Jersey patch (partner, industry, start, term, annual, total, expires); MMR (partner, start, term, annual, total, expires); Apparel (partner, start, term, annual, total, expires); Institutional (undergraduate enrollment, total enrollment, enrollment year, varsity sports); Performance (FB wins and losses 2021-25 and win % [formula], MBB wins and losses 2021-22 to 2025-26 and win % [formula], Directors' Cup rank and points 2024-25 and 2025-26); Academic (FB multi-year APR, department GSR, FB GSR, academic data year).
- Deal-value rule: in each annual/term/total triplet, hardcode what was reported, derive the third by formula, "--" otherwise; never two formulas in one triplet.

## Hard rules
- USA Today's coaches' pay tables are allowed for head coach pay (analyst's decision of 13 November 2026, reversing the earlier ban); `build_usatoday.py` uses them only for cells no other source fills, and their figures are one season behind. Never cite Wikipedia for a number. Every hardcoded value must trace to a specific URL and be logged in `Source Log`. Never guess, never compute a figure that was not reported (no escalator math, no averages presented as a year figure, no inferred end years). "--" with a reason in the Source Log where nothing can be sourced.
- Financial source hierarchy: Sportico MFRS compilation (or the school's own posted MFRS report when newer); EADA only for the 15 schools with no public MFRS, flagged as EADA in the basis and data type columns; never Knight-Newhouse (unreachable from the build environment), never Sportico-free compilations.
- Coaching pay is one column per sport: total guaranteed annual pay for the current season (base salary plus supplemental compensation; contract average where that is all a source gives). Retention payments, signing bonuses and performance bonuses are excluded and described in the Source Log note (analyst's decision of 13 November 2026, after a three-column layout was tried and dropped). Stated escalators are not applied.
- Verification: every researched value carries a status in the Source Log's Verification column (Source data file, Found on source page, Re-read and confirmed, Analyst classification, Not re-verified). A value stays only if a page states it: no sums of contract components, no averages as a year figure, no end year derived from a term, nothing attributed to a newspaper-compiled salary database. Unsupported values are withdrawn through `data/research_withdrawals.csv` with the reason kept in the Source Log. See the Verification section of README.md for the scripts.
- Relevance: anything older than about five years must still be in force to stay. Deals whose recorded term has ended are refreshed or blanked; `qa.py` lists any that remain. The expires column takes a stated end year where a source gives one; a start year may be the announcement year where no effective date is stated (noted in the Source Log); coach contract end is the stated expiry year.

## Style (follow exactly; modeled on my KSU diligence workbook)
- Font Aptos Narrow 9 everywhere; B2 brand line "SMASHSPORTS" in Montserrat 11 with "SMASH" bold and "SPORTS" regular, on every tab; B3 "Power 4 Athletic Department Benchmarking" in bold; nothing in B4 or B5. Column A width 1 (gutter), column B width 2, data starts in column C, header in row 6, data from row 7.
- Header row: black fill, white centered text, thin top and bottom borders, row height 24. Section bands on lookup and school tabs: light gray E8E8E8, bold, thin top and bottom borders. Average rows: gray D9D9D9, bold, thin top border; Median rows: same with double bottom border.
- Font colors: blue for hardcoded figures (underlined where, and only where, a source hyperlink is attached), green for links to another tab, black for formulas and for identity text (school, conference, public or private, state). No yellow fills except the single input cell on School Lookup. No italics, no merged cells, no wrapped text, no subtitle lines. Gridlines off; panes frozen under the header and right of the first column.
- Hyperlinks: none on identity columns, fiscal year, totals, breakouts or line items; the financial source hyperlink sits only on the Financial source basis cell. Other sourced hardcodes (coach names, venue facts, deal terms) keep their hyperlinks.
- Number formats: `#,##0_);\(#,##0\);"-"??_);_(@_)` for dollars and counts (accounting layout without the `* ` fill, which would be underlined across the whole cell on hyperlinked values), 0.0% for percentages, 0.000 for win %, 0 for years. Numbers stored as numbers; "--" shown wherever there is nothing to display; arithmetic on cells that could hold "--" is guarded with ISNUMBER.
- QA before handing back: 68 rows across the conference and Independent tabs and on All Schools, no per-school tabs, zero formula errors after LibreOffice recalc, no text-stored numerics, triplet rule holds, USA Today cited for coach pay only, 10 random hardcodes re-verified against their sources.
