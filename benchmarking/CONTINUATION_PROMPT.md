# Continue the Power 4 athletic department benchmarking workbook

Repository: wondimagegndawit/sportsnews, branch `claude/sleepy-hamilton-613gvu`, folder `benchmarking/`. Work on that branch (develop, commit, push there). Do not create a pull request unless I ask.

## What exists
- `Power4_Athletic_Department_Benchmarking.xlsx`: a finished, formula-driven analyst workbook benchmarking the 53 Power 4 athletic departments that have public NCAA MFRS financial data (ACC 9, Big Ten 16, Big 12 13, SEC 15). The 13 private universities and Pittsburgh are deliberately excluded (no public MFRS; EADA is not comparable).
- Tabs (names carry no number prefix): `Read Me`, `All Schools` (all formulas linking to the conference tabs, plus average, median and count blocks by conference and for all schools, one block per fiscal year and one for all years, and a Data breakdown table of school counts by conference), `ACC`, `Big Ten`, `Big 12`, `SEC` (the only tabs with hardcoded values, one row per school, nine summary rows at the bottom: Average, Median and Count (schools with a value), each for every fiscal year in the data and for all years, followed by a Data breakdown table counting schools by fiscal year reported and by financial source basis; fiscal-year rows cover the MFRS financial columns only, with the year held in the Fiscal year column as the filter), `Source Log` (one row per sourced value: school, conference, metric, value, unit, fiscal year, source type, source name, URL, date accessed, note), `School Lookup` (yellow input cell with data validation, INDEX/MATCH list), then one tab per school (INDEX/MATCH on the conference tab, with a conference-average column and a guarded difference column).
- Metric columns, in order: Identity (School, Conference, Public or Private, State); Financial (Fiscal year, Financial source basis, Total operating revenue, Football revenue, Men's basketball revenue, Other sports revenue [formula], Non-program-specific revenue, Total operating expenses, Operating surplus [formula], Media rights, Ticket sales, Contributions, NCAA and conference distributions, Direct institutional support, Indirect institutional support, Student fees, Total subsidy [formula = direct + student fees + indirect], Subsidy as % of revenue [formula], Total coaching salaries and benefits, Total athletics-related debt, Annual debt service); Coaching (FB and MBB head coach name, annual guaranteed compensation, contract end year, pay source type); Venues (stadium name, capacity, year opened, last renovation; arena name, capacity, year opened); Attendance (football from the D1.ticker FBS Attendance Trends pages: average home attendance 2023, 2024, 2025, year-over-year change 2025, 5-year average, change vs 5-year average, % of capacity 2025, all as reported; men's basketball from the NCAA Men's Basketball Attendance Records PDF, 2025-26: home games, total home attendance, average home attendance, % of capacity [formula = average / arena capacity], and four top-30-only columns from the NCAA leader tables: all games played, all-games attendance, 2024-25 average and change in average, with no average or median); Stadium naming rights and Arena naming rights (partner, industry, deal type, start year, term, annual value, total value, expires [formula]); Jersey patch (partner, industry, start, term, annual, total, expires); MMR (partner, start, term, annual, total, expires); Apparel (partner, start, term, annual, total, expires); Institutional (undergraduate enrollment, total enrollment, enrollment year, varsity sports); Performance (FB wins and losses 2021-25 and win % [formula], MBB wins and losses 2021-22 to 2025-26 and win % [formula], Directors' Cup rank and points 2024-25 and 2025-26); Academic (FB multi-year APR, department GSR, FB GSR, academic data year).
- Deal-value rule: in each annual/term/total triplet, hardcode what was reported, derive the third by formula, "--" otherwise; never two formulas in one triplet.

## Hard rules
- Never use USA Today / sportsdata.usatoday.com for anything. Never cite Wikipedia for a number. Every hardcoded value must trace to a specific URL and be logged in `Source Log`. Never guess, never compute a figure that was not reported (no escalator math, no averages presented as a year figure, no inferred end years). "--" with a reason in the Source Log where nothing can be sourced.
- Financial source hierarchy: Sportico MFRS compilation (or the school's own posted MFRS report when newer), never EADA, never Knight-Newhouse (unreachable from the build environment), never Sportico-free compilations.
- Coaching pay = guaranteed annual compensation for the current season under the current contract, year-specific.

## Style (follow exactly; modeled on my KSU diligence workbook)
- Font Aptos Narrow 9 everywhere; B2 brand line "SMASHSPORTS" in Montserrat 11 with "SMASH" bold and "SPORTS" regular, on every tab; B3 "Power 4 Athletic Department Benchmarking" in bold; nothing in B4 or B5. Column A width 1 (gutter), column B width 2, data starts in column C, header in row 6, data from row 7.
- Header row: black fill, white centered text, thin top and bottom borders, row height 24. Section bands on lookup and school tabs: light gray E8E8E8, bold, thin top and bottom borders. Average rows: gray D9D9D9, bold, thin top border; Median rows: same with double bottom border.
- Font colors: blue for hardcoded figures (never underlined, including where a source hyperlink is attached), green for links to another tab, black for formulas and for identity text (school, conference, public or private, state). No yellow fills except the single input cell on School Lookup. No italics, no merged cells, no wrapped text, no subtitle lines. Gridlines off; panes frozen under the header and right of the first column.
- Hyperlinks: none on identity columns, fiscal year, totals, breakouts or line items; the financial source hyperlink sits only on the Financial source basis cell. Other sourced hardcodes (coach names, venue facts, deal terms) keep their hyperlinks.
- Number formats: accounting `_(* #,##0_);_(* \(#,##0\);_(* "-"??_);_(@_)` for dollars and counts, 0.0% for percentages, 0.000 for win %, 0 for years. Numbers stored as numbers; "--" shown wherever there is nothing to display; arithmetic on cells that could hold "--" is guarded with ISNUMBER.
- QA before handing back: 53 rows on every conference total and All Schools, 53 school tabs, zero formula errors after LibreOffice recalc, no text-stored numerics, triplet rule holds, no USA Today anywhere, 10 random hardcodes re-verified against their sources.
