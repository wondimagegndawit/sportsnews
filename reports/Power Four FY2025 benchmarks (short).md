**On average, how much did Power 4 athletic departments burn last year (FY2025)?**

- Before donations and university support, the average public Power Four department lost $62.9M in FY2025 (SEC −$78.1M, ACC −$71.6M, Big 12 −$57.4M, Big Ten −$48.2M). Boosters, campus subsidies and endowment income filled the hole, leaving an average bottom line of roughly zero.
  - Source links: [nil-ncaa.com, FY2025 (52 public Power Four schools)](https://nil-ncaa.com/); [Drake Group appendix to Congress, Aug 2026](https://www.thedrakegroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FINL-APPENDIX-B-08.21.26.pdf)
- Counting donations but not university money or student fees, only 14 of the 53 public Power Four departments covered their expenses in FY2025; 20 more were in the black only because of campus support, and 19 lost money outright. Ten years ago only seven were losing money.
  - Source links: [Sen. Cantwell report, Sept 2026](https://www.cantwell.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/spiraling_college_sports_costs_snapshot2.pdf); [Cardinal News analysis of Knight-Newhouse, Sept 2026](https://cardinalnews.org/2026/09/04/college-sports-are-awash-in-more-money-than-ever-yet-a-growing-number-of-schools-are-losing-money-on-intercollegiate-athletics/)
- FY2025 is pre-House; the $20.5M revenue share started July 1, 2025. First FY2026 results: LSU −$44M, Colorado −$27M, Kansas −$15.8M, Louisville −$12.5M, and Michigan balanced only with $15M from the university.
  - Source links: [FootballScoop on LSU, Sept 2026](https://www.footballscoop.com/2026/09/24/lsu-athletics-projects-26-million-deficit-after-44-million-deficit-last-year-president-says-program-must-stop-that-bleeding); [USA Today on Colorado](https://www.aol.com/articles/colorado-financial-report-shows-limits-095241132.html); [Kansas City Star, Aug 2026](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/ku-athletics-board-approves-13-103000239.html); [WDRB on Louisville](https://www.wdrb.com/sports/louisville-athletics-projects-a-12-5-million-deficit-in-1st-budget-of-revenue-sharing-era/article_071eb72d-f599-45c0-90fe-26af47608af9.html); [MGoBlue, June 2025](https://mgoblue.com/news/2025/6/12/general-michigan-athletics-projects-balanced-budget-for-fiscal-year-2026)

**On average, what percentage of Power 4 athletic department revenue is donations?**

- About a quarter. The NCAA puts donor contributions and endowments at 26% of total revenue for the 69 autonomy schools in FY2024, the second-largest source after media and conference money (31%) and ahead of tickets (17%).
  - Source link: [NCAA 10-year trends, Dec 2025, p. 16](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/research/Finances/2025D1RES_DI-RevExpReport_Final.pdf)
- In FY2025, public Power Four schools averaged $44.4M in booster contributions on $183.1M of total revenue, or 24%. The SEC leans hardest on donors ($65.8M per school) and the Big Ten least ($36.0M), because its media money is the largest.
  - Source links: [nil-ncaa.com, FY2025](https://nil-ncaa.com/); [Drake Group appendix, Aug 2026](https://www.thedrakegroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FINL-APPENDIX-B-08.21.26.pdf)
- Donations are still growing despite NIL collectives: Ohio State took a record $68.3M (up from $52.5M), Oklahoma a record $92.9M, and Auburn $56.4M (up $11.1M) in FY2025.
  - Source links: [Ohio State News, Jan 2026](https://news.osu.edu/ohio-state-athletics-sets-record-with-336m-in-fy25-revenue-after-national-championship-season/); [247Sports on Oklahoma, Jan 2026](https://247sports.com/college/oklahoma/article/oklahoma-ncaa-financial-report-for-2025-fiscal-year-sooners-first-in-sec-272740382/); [AL.com on Auburn, Jan 2026](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/much-money-did-auburn-athletics-222336441.html)

**On average, what revenue do Power 4 athletic departments have?**

- Public Power Four departments averaged $183.1M of total revenue in FY2025 against $182.9M of expenses. Average expenses by conference: SEC $224.9M, Big Ten $194.2M, ACC $169.3M, Big 12 $131.7M.
  - Source links: [nil-ncaa.com, FY2025](https://nil-ncaa.com/); [Drake Group appendix, Aug 2026](https://www.thedrakegroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FINL-APPENDIX-B-08.21.26.pdf)
- The NCAA's full-sample median, including the 15 private schools that don't publish, was $149.3M in FY2024 (range $88M to $332M), and the median school was 89% self-sufficient. The top of the table in FY2025: Texas $352.5M, Ohio State $336.1M, Tennessee $304.1M, Michigan $275.8M.
  - Source links: [NCAA 10-year trends, Dec 2025, pp. 7 and 45](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/research/Finances/2025D1RES_DI-RevExpReport_Final.pdf); [Sportico on Texas](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/college-sports/2026/texas-longhorns-spending-fy25-1234882184/); [Ohio State News](https://news.osu.edu/ohio-state-athletics-sets-record-with-336m-in-fy25-revenue-after-national-championship-season/); [Tennessee FY25 NCAA report](https://utsports.com/documents/download/2026/1/15/FY25_NCAA_AUP.pdf); [Extra Points on Michigan](https://www.extrapointsmb.com/p/special-report-a-deep-dive-into-the-michigan-athletic-department-s-finances)
- Revenue growth has not kept pace with spending: over ten years the median Power school's generated revenue rose 56% while expenses rose 81%. The biggest single line is the conference check, $76.1M per Big Ten school and $72.4M per SEC school in FY2025, versus about $47M in the ACC and $39.5M in the Big 12.
  - Source links: [NCAA 10-year trends, Dec 2025, p. 36](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/research/Finances/2025D1RES_DI-RevExpReport_Final.pdf); [CBS Sports on Big Ten, May 2026](https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/news/big-ten-1-37-billion-in-revenue-to-its-18-members-for-2025-fiscal-year/); [SEC release, Feb 2026](https://www.secsports.com/news/2026/02/sec-announces-2024-25-revenue-distribution); [Wilner, Bay Area News Group, May 2026](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2026/05/25/big-12-spring-meetings-financial-reports-revenue-deficit/)

**On average, how many sports do Power 4 athletic departments sponsor?**

- About 22. Per the NCAA membership directory for 2026-27, the 68 Power Four schools average 21.7 sports (median 21), from 16 at Kansas State, UCF and Mississippi State to 36 at Stanford and Ohio State; by conference, Big Ten 23.9, ACC 23.6, SEC 20.1, Big 12 18.8.
  - Source links: [NCAA membership directory](https://web3.ncaa.org/directory/); [Stanford Facts](https://facts.stanford.edu/campus-life/cardinal-athletics)
- The floor is 16: FBS members must sponsor at least 16 varsity sports including football, with no waivers allowed, and 17 Power Four schools already sit at 16 to 18. Cutting sports is not a real lever for most of them.
  - Source link: [NCAA Division I Manual 2026-27, Bylaws 20.9.9.1 and 20.9.9.5](https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008)
- Only football and men's basketball make money; the NCAA's last sport-level data showed every other sport losing $0.2M to $3.2M at the median. No Power Four school has dropped a sport because of the House settlement.
  - Source links: [NCAA Revenues and Expenses 2004-2016, Table 4.9](https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/research/Finances/2017D1RES_D1RevExpReportFinal.pdf); [Utah Athletics, Apr 2025](https://utahutes.com/news/2025/4/29/utah-athletics-to-discontinue-beach-volleyball-program-at-conclusion-of-academic-year); [Iowa State Athletics, Apr 2026](https://cyclones.com/news/2026/4/16/athletics-iowa-state-adds-womens-wrestling-as-18th-varsity-program)
