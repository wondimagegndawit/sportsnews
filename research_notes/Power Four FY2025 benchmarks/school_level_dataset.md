# Power Four FY2025 athletic department finances: school-level dataset (NCAA MFRS basis)

Research date: 2026-10-27. Fiscal year 2025 = July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025 for every school in the table (no Power Four public school in the dataset uses an Aug. 31 year-end for its NCAA report; the SEC conference office itself uses Aug. 31, which is irrelevant here). All dollar figures in $ millions unless stated; raw dollar-exact values are in the source pages linked in each row.

## Key Question 1: What are each public Power Four school's FY2025 total revenue, expenses, net result, contributions, endowment income and allocated support (with source URL)?

### Takeaway
FY2025 NCAA Membership Financial Reporting System (MFRS) totals were obtained for 52 of the 53 public Power Four athletic departments (all except Georgia Tech): 48 with full line-item detail from the College Sports Finance (CSF) compilation of MFRS filings, 1 from the primary NCAA report PDF (Indiana), and 3 from newspaper reports of the MFRS filing (Auburn, Florida, Rutgers). Spot checks of 13 schools against primary PDFs or independent press reports matched the CSF totals to the dollar in every case except Alabama's expenses (a $1.0M discrepancy with AL.com).

## Key Question 4: Methods, definitions and source-reliability notes

### Takeaway
All table figures are NCAA MFRS "Total Operating Revenues" (sum of categories 1-19, which includes allocated revenue) and "Total Operating Expenses" (categories 20-44) for the year ended June 30, 2025, as compiled from public-records copies; where a university budget/audit figure also exists it is noted but not used.

### Cited Findings
- MFRS definitions used: Total Operating Revenues = "Total of Categories 1-19"; Total Operating Expenses = "Total of Categories 20-44"; category 2 = Direct State or Other Government Support; 3 = Student Fees; 4 = Direct Institutional Support; 5 = Less - Transfers to Institution; 6 = Indirect Institutional Support; 6A = Indirect Institutional Support - Athletic Facilities Debt Service, Lease and Rental Fees; 8 = Contributions ("cash and marketable securities"); 9 = In-Kind; 17 = Athletics Restricted Endowment and Investment Income ("spending policy distributions from athletics restricted endowments") — [Utah FY25 NCAA report PDF](https://utahutes.com/documents/download/2026/1/21/FY25_NCAA_Revenue_and_Expense_Report.pdf) and [IU 2025 NCAA FRS report PDF](https://npr.brightspotcdn.com/f8/cd/6f6273e141998c6ff6b63178b250/2025-ncaa-frs-report.pdf). Allocated support in this file = categories 2 + 3 + 4 + 6 + 6A (category 5 transfers-back are already netted inside MFRS totals and are not separately subtracted; South Carolina's ($5,003,749) is the one such case seen).
- FY2025 is the last year before House-settlement revenue sharing (effective July 1, 2025); Knight-Newhouse notes few institutions reported direct athlete payments for FY2025, and the new "Institutional NIL Revenue Share" category totaled $52.93M across 110 public FBS schools — [Knight-Newhouse FY2025 sheet](https://www.knightcommission.org/wp-content/uploads/KNAD_database_information_FY2025.pdf). Penn State reported $18.4M of institutional NIL revenue share in FY2025 — [SwimSwam](https://swimswam.com/penn-state-pays-out-18-4-million-in-institutional-nil-revenue-share-none-went-to-swim-dive/); Ohio State reported none — [SwimSwam](https://swimswam.com/ohio-state-athletics-posts-record-revenue-with-336-million-fy2025-no-nil-institutional-share/).
