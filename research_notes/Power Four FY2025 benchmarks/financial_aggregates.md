# Power Four FY2025 Financial Benchmarks: Revenue, Expenses, Burn, Subsidy, Donations

Scope notes that apply to everything below:
- "FY2025" = July 1, 2024 – June 30, 2025 (the last fiscal year before House-settlement revenue sharing began July 1, 2025). "FY2024" = July 1, 2023 – June 30, 2024.
- Power Four (P4) membership used by all FY2025 sources is post-realignment (Texas/Oklahoma in SEC; USC/UCLA/Oregon/Washington in Big Ten; Stanford/Cal/SMU in ACC; Arizona/Arizona State/Colorado/Utah in Big 12). Notre Dame is an FBS independent (ACC in other sports) and is private; Oregon State and Washington State are Pac-12 and are NOT in any P4 sample below.
- Private P4 members do not file public NCAA MFRS reports, so every FY2025 "public-only" sample excludes roughly 15-16 schools: SEC: Vanderbilt. Big Ten: Northwestern, USC. Big 12: Baylor, BYU, TCU. ACC: Boston College, Duke, Miami, Notre Dame (if counted), SMU, Stanford, Syracuse, Wake Forest. Pittsburgh (state-related) and Penn State are exempt from Pennsylvania open-records law; Penn State voluntarily releases its report and appears in most FY2025 samples, Pitt does not. That leaves 52-53 reporting P4 publics (SEC 14, Big Ten 16, Big 12 13, ACC 9-10).
- Two accessibility problems: (1) knightnewhousedata.org (including the four conference custom reports Sen. Cantwell cites) and Sportico article pages returned HTTP 403 / Tollbit redirects to my tools, so Knight-Newhouse conference medians and Sportico's own FY2025 aggregate articles are cited only via secondary reporting that quotes them. (2) The Knight Commission FY2025 one-pager PDF was readable and is used directly.

## Key Question 1: FY2025 Power Four revenue (median / average), with FY2024 comparison and NCAA FY2024 confirmation

### Takeaway
No primary source publishes a single "P4 median total revenue" number for FY2025; the best available FY2025 P4 figures are a 52-school public-only dataset (nil-ncaa.com, built from NCAA MFRS filings and endorsed in the Drake Group's August 2026 Congressional appendix) showing average total revenue-and-support of $183.1M and (my calculation from its school-level rows) median total revenue of about $175.7M, versus the NCAA's own FY2024 all-69-school (public + private) Autonomy medians of $149.3M total revenue and $133.8M generated revenue.

### Cited Findings

**NCAA FY2024 (latest NCAA aggregate; confirmed from the report itself)**
- Report: NCAA Research, "Division I Athletics Finances: 10-Year Trends from 2015 to 2024," December 2025 (84 pages). Covers NCAA Membership Financial Reporting System data for FY2014-15 through FY2023-24; "352 active Division I institutions provided data." ### Inferences
- Derived from the 52 nil-ncaa.com school rows (my arithmetic; total revenue = operating revenue + contributions + fees/school support + endowment, which reconciles exactly to reported totals, e.g., Ohio State $336.08M, Michigan $275.84M): FY2025 P4 public mean total revenue $182.7M, median $175.7M, sum $9.50B; mean expenses $182.5M, median $175.9M, sum $9.49B. Conference medians (total revenue / expenses): SEC (n=14) $213.4M / $211.5M; Big Ten (n=16) $181.7M / $183.5M; ACC (n=9) $156.1M / $162.1M; Big 12 (n=13) $140.3M / $135.6M. Caveat: nil-ncaa.com's operating expenses for Penn State, Oklahoma State and Louisville exclude revenue-share payments reported on their FY2025 filings (Penn State's full MFRS expense figure is $254.6M vs $236.3M in this table), and its Texas expense figure ($372.65M) is $3.2M below Sportico's $375.9M, so expense medians are slightly understated.
- The FY2025 public-only P4 median total revenue (~$176M) is roughly $26M above the NCAA's FY2024 all-member autonomy median ($149.3M); a principal should treat the NCAA figure as the only true all-69-school benchmark and the FY2025 public figures as a higher-revenue subset.

## Key Question 2: FY2025 burn — net result excluding allocated support, net after allocated support, and the subsidy dollars

### Takeaway
On an NCAA-style basis (excluding student fees and institutional/government support), only 14 of 53 publicly reporting P4 departments covered expenses in FY2025 per Knight-Newhouse (vs. 20 of 69 in FY2024 per GAO/NCAA, median gap −$15.2M); after counting subsidies, 34 of 53 showed a surplus and 19 a deficit, and the average P4 public school's allocated support was ~$15M (total ~$770M across 52 schools), with the four conferences' public members collectively close to break-even (+$0.2M average) only because of $44M average donations plus that support.

### Takeaway
