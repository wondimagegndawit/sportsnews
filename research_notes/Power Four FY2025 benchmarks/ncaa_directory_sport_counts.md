## Key Question 1: How many NCAA-sponsored varsity sports does each of the 68 Power Four schools sponsor in the current (2026-27) academic year, per a primary source?

### Takeaway
All 68 schools were sourced from the NCAA membership directory (web3.ncaa.org/directory), whose per-school page lists "Sponsored Sports for the 2026-2027 Academic Year"; counts range from 16 (Kansas State, UCF, Mississippi State) to 37 (Stanford), with an overall mean of 21.7 and median of 21. No school was left blank; no Wikipedia, EADA or estimates were used.

### Inferences

- The EADA-based mean of 18.1 / median 17 from the prior pass understates the NCAA counting basis by roughly 3.5 sports per school on average (21.7 / 21 here); the gap is almost entirely the cross country / indoor track / outdoor track split (up to +4 per school with both men's and women's programs) plus non-EADA entries such as mixed rifle.
- Directory counts match the official-site counts where the site and directory use the same granularity (Georgia Tech, Mississippi State, Nebraska); the one-sport differences at Stanford (sailing split coed/women's) and Ohio State (unreconciled) are counting-convention differences, not missing programs.
- If only sports with NCAA championships are wanted, subtract: Cal men's rugby (1), Stanford mixed+women's sailing and women's squash (3), BC men's/women's sailing (2), Virginia men's/women's squash (2), Ohio State mixed pistol and synchronized swimming (2), Stanford synchronized swimming (1). That would move Stanford to 33, Ohio State to 33, Cal to 29, BC to 29, Virginia to 25 and lower the overall mean by about 0.16.

### Gaps

- Ohio State's official 36 vs. the directory's 35 could not be reconciled because ohiostatebuckeyes.com returned only a title to the fetch tool; the most likely explanation is that Ohio State counts a sport the directory folds into a mixed entry (e.g., rifle or pistol by gender) but this is unverified.
- The directory reflects what each school's compliance office entered; two pages had duplicate rows (Pitt, Auburn) which were removed, but the directory could also omit a newly added program if the school has not yet entered it (none were detected for 2026-27 beyond Iowa State women's wrestling, which does not begin until 2027-28).

## Key Question 2: What is the counting basis, and are emerging and non-championship sports included?

### Takeaway
The directory counts men's and women's cross country, indoor track and outdoor track as six separate sports (matching the Bylaw 20.9 sport-sponsorship basis), counts rifle/pistol/sailing as single "Mixed" entries where a school fields a coed team, and includes NCAA emerging sports (beach volleyball, acrobatics & tumbling, equestrian, triathlon, stunt, flag football, women's wrestling) as well as a few non-NCAA varsity sports (rugby, squash, sailing, synchronized swimming, pistol) when the school lists them.

### Inferences

- Because the directory is populated by each school for NCAA sports-sponsorship reporting, it is the closest available public proxy for the Bylaw 20.9 count; the inclusion of a handful of non-championship sports (rugby, squash, sailing, synchro, pistol) means it is slightly above a strict championship-sports count for about six schools.
- Conference averages are driven by membership composition: the ACC (Stanford, BC, Cal, UNC, Duke, Virginia, Notre Dame) and Big Ten (Ohio State, Penn State, Michigan) carry the broad-based private and flagship programs, while the Big 12 (mean 18.8) is populated mostly by schools at or near the 16-sport Division I FBS minimum.

### Gaps

- The directory does not flag which listed sports are NCAA championship sports versus emerging or non-NCAA sports; the classification above is based on sport names and NCAA emerging-sports status and was not confirmed against a separate NCAA list.
- Official athletics-site counts for the other 62 schools were not collected (sites are Sidearm-hosted and blocked automated fetches); the directory was used as the single primary source as instructed.

## Methods note

- Source: NCAA membership directory (https://web3.ncaa.org/directory/). Org IDs were taken from the directory's Division I member list API (memberList?type=12&division=I), filtered to the four conferences; all 68 schools resolved. Each orgDetail page was downloaded on 2026-10-27 and the table under "Sponsored Sports for the 2026-2027 Academic Year" was parsed; each distinct sport row counts as one sport.
- Academic year: the directory's current cycle is 2026-27 (academicYear 2027 in the API); counts therefore reflect sports sponsored in 2026-27, the current academic year as of the access date.
- Counting basis: cross country, indoor track and outdoor track counted separately (as the directory lists them); coed rifle/pistol/sailing count once each; baseball, football, softball and field hockey are single-gender sports and were allocated to M or W accordingly in the M/W/X split. Duplicate rows (Pitt swimming & diving x2, Auburn softball x2) were removed.
- Future additions not yet competing are excluded (Iowa State women's wrestling, 2027-28). Nebraska women's flag football is included because its first season is spring 2027, within 2026-27.
- Raw parsed data (per-school sport lists with head coach, division and conference affiliation) saved in the session scratchpad at /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-sportsnews/e7520494-3371-5adc-8f6a-4fc26ebd4b31/scratchpad/parsed2.json; downloaded HTML pages in the same directory under dl/orgs/.
