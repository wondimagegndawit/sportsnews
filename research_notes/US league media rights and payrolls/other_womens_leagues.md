## Key Question 1: PWHL (binational US/Canada) -- salary structure, 2025-26 total payroll, 2026-27 figures, media rights

### Takeaway
The PWHLPA's first public salary disclosure (May 26, 2026) shows the eight-team league spent $11,110,938.65 on 2025-26 base salaries (194 contracts, ~$1.39M per team). The CBA (through 2031) has no traditional hard cap or individual max; instead each team must hit a team-average salary target ($58,349.50 in 2025-26; $60,099.99 in 2026-27) with a 3%/yr-rising minimum ($37,131.50 in 2025-26; $38,245.45 in 2026-27). No rights fees have ever been disclosed; 2025-26 had no national US TV deal (free YouTube plus regional RSN/OTA), and the first season-long national US TV package (Scripps/ION, 2026-27) was announced Sept 9, 2026 with no financial terms.

### Cited Findings
Salary structure and payroll:
- League-wide 2025-26 player salary spend was $11,110,938.65 (base salaries only), per the PWHLPA "Player Salary Guide" released May 26, 2026; 17 players made the minimum — [The IX / The Ice Garden](https://www.theixsports.com/the-ice-garden/pwhl/pwhlpa-releases-2025-26-salary-data-pwhl-womens-hockey/)
- Per-team 2025-26 base-salary totals (reported): Boston Fleet $1,436,173.50 (25 contracts, avg $57,446.94); Minnesota Frost $1,409,038.50 (24, $58,709.94); Montréal Victoire $1,446,300.00 (25, $57,852.00); New York Sirens $1,372,301.50 (25, $54,892.06); Ottawa Charge $1,414,301.50 (24, $58,929.23); Seattle Torrent $1,357,484.50 (24, $56,561.85); Toronto Sceptres $1,347,429.65 (24, $56,142.90); Vancouver Goldeneyes $1,327,909.50 (23, $57,735.20). Highest salary: Emily Clark (Ottawa) $126,090; Sarah Fillier (NY) $125,000 — [The IX / The Ice Garden](https://www.theixsports.com/the-ice-garden/pwhl/pwhlpa-releases-2025-26-salary-data-pwhl-womens-hockey/)
- 2025-26 league minimum $37,131.50 (up from $35,000 in 2024); minimum rises 3% per year of the CBA, which runs through 2031, so 2026-27 minimum = $38,245.45 — [Yahoo Sports / Boston Globe](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/pwhl-players-association-releases-salaries-180106170.html); [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/05/26/sports/pwhl-player-salaries/)
- Instead of a cap, each team's average salary must reach a target: $58,349.50 in 2025-26, rising to $60,099.99 in 2026-27; the average may vary by up to 10% for player movement; "There are no maximum salaries in the CBA" and no traditional salary cap — [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/pwhl-players-association-releases-salaries-180106170.html); [The IX](https://www.theixsports.com/the-ice-garden/pwhl/pwhlpa-releases-2025-26-salary-data-pwhl-womens-hockey/)

- Conflicting older framing: a NESN explainer describes a "$1.3 million team cap," $35,000 minimum, and a requirement that at least six players per team earn $80,000+; Wikipedia similarly says each team must sign at least six players at $80,000+ and no more than nine at the league minimum. These reflect launch-era (2023-24) reporting; the PWHLPA's 2026 materials describe the mechanism as an average-salary target, not a cap — [NESN](https://nesn.com/pwhl/news/how-pwhl-teams-are-built-roster-rules-salary-cap-basics-draft-system/aa6450784cd9a3b306957604); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Women's_Hockey_League)
- Roster size for 2026-27 was still undecided as of Oct 14, 2026: the league operated its first three seasons with 23 signed players plus 3 reserves per team and is weighing 23/24/25-player formats with or without reserves; The Hockey News notes any change "will have salary cap implications" and a 24th player would be near league minimum — [The Hockey News](https://thehockeynews.com/womens/pwhl/pwhl-has-yet-to-announce-2026-27-roster-size-with-camps-closing-in); [The Hockey News](https://thehockeynews.com/womens/pwhl/pwhl-offseason-roster-size-and-reserve-changes-possible-summer-leagues-and-national-team-competitions)

### Inferences
- 2025-26 per-team average payroll (base) = $11,110,938.65 / 8 = $1,388,867 (my arithmetic from PWHLPA data). This is consistent with the average-target mechanism: $58,349.50 x 23 contracts = $1,342,039; x 25 = $1,458,738.
- 2026-27 total payroll proxy (not yet published): 12 teams x $60,099.99 target x 23 players = $16,587,597; at 24 players = $17,308,797; at 25 players = $18,029,997. Alternative proxy using 2025-26 actual per-team average ($1.389M) x 12 = $16.67M. Label: estimate.
- Media rights AAV: effectively undisclosed/likely minimal. Every US arrangement to date (YouTube, RSNs, OTA, Scripps FAST/ION) is described as distribution; no outlet has reported a US rights fee, and the league's own executive declined to rule out paying for airtime. Canadian fees (TSN/Sportsnet/CBC/Prime) are also undisclosed. Treat combined AAV as "not reported."

### Gaps
- The CBA PDF itself (pwhlpa.com) was not fetched; the "10% variance" and "no max" details come from Yahoo/Globe/The IX reporting on the PWHLPA guide.
- No 2026-27 per-team payroll yet (season starts Dec 5, 2026; roster size unresolved as of mid-Oct 2026).
- No reported value for any PWHL media deal, Canadian or US; no confirmation of whether the league pays production/time-buy costs on Scripps or RSNs.
- Hollywood Reporter's 2025-26 TV-rights explainer was paywalled (HTTP 402) and not read.

## Key Question 2: Unrivaled (3x3 basketball) -- 2025/2026 pay, total compensation, TNT deal, valuation

### Takeaway
Unrivaled's 2025 season paid 36 players a reported average of ~$220,000 (league's own figure $222,222; FOS has also cited "$200,000"/"$7M+"), implying a ~$7.9M payroll; 2026 expanded to 8 teams/48 rostered players plus a 6-player development pool with salaries "going up" but no new average published. The TNT Sports deal (Oct 2024) is a guaranteed three-year deal with a three-year WBD option reportedly worth ~$100M over six years (SBJ via secondary sources), with WBD holding equity; valuation rose from $340M (Series B, Sept 2025) to $650M (Series C >$100M, Aug 2026), with players owning ~30%.

### Cited Findings
Pay:
- 2025 average salary reportedly $220,000, no player under six figures; co-founder Alex Bazzell said 2026 numbers "are going up" even with more players, but did not confirm a new average; Paige Bueckers reportedly >$350,000 for 2026 — [Front Office Sports (Aug 4, 2025)](https://frontofficesports.com/unrivaled-has-signed-nearly-90-of-2026-roster-salaries-to-increase/)
- League claimed "highest average salary in women's sports history at $222,222" — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/01/15/unrivaled-womens-basketball-wnba-teams-schedule)
- FOS (Annie Costabile, Apr 6, 2025, as summarized by SI): first-year revenue over $27 million; "$7 million or more on player salaries"; average $200,000 across 36 founding players; all year-one contracts doubled as injury insurance covering a player's WNBA salary — [SI](https://www.si.com/onsi/womens-fastbreak/news/unrivaled-revenue-injury-insurance-details-from-inaugural-season-revealed-01jr67mtbsfx)
- 2026 season: 8 teams including expansion Breeze BC and Hive BC; six rostered players per team (48) plus a six-player development pool; Jan 5-Mar 4, 2026; every player earns a minimum of six figures; 1-on-1 tournament prize pool $350,000 ($200k winner) — [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/wnba/news/2026-unrivaled-preview-rosters-expansion-schedule-pay-season-two/)

### Inferences
- 2025 payroll proxy: 36 x $220,000 = $7.92M (FOS/CBS average) or 36 x $222,222 = $8.0M (league figure); FOS's April 2025 "$7M+"/$200k-average framing gives $7.2M. The "$8M+ with equity" figure in the assignment is consistent with these but I found no primary source stating "$8M" exactly (a secondary site claims "$8.65 million" without attribution; not relied on).
- 2026 payroll proxy: salaries rose and headcount went to 48 rostered + 6 development = 54. Floor: 54 x $100,000 = $5.4M (every player guaranteed six figures). Using the 2025 average as a lower bound for 2026 average: 48 x $220,000 = $10.56M; 54 x $220,000 = $11.88M. Best estimate "> $10.5M" (estimate; no reported total).
- Media AAV: ~$100M / 6 years = ~$16.7M per year if WBD exercises the option (SBJ-sourced, via secondary); guaranteed portion is three years, value unstated. This is the only league in this set with a reported eight-figure annual rights value.

### Gaps
- No published 2026 or 2027 average salary or total compensation pool; Bazzell repeatedly declined to give numbers.
- Guaranteed (first three-year) value of the TNT deal not reported separately; original SBJ article not fetched directly.
- Series C exact amount beyond "more than $100 million" not disclosed.

## Key Question 4: Pro Volleyball Federation -> Major League Volleyball (MLV) -- status, salaries, cap, media

### Takeaway
PVF did not pause; it merged into Major League Volleyball (announced Aug 4-5, 2025; PVF raised $40M; combined valuation >$325M per Sportico/SportsPro) and played the 2026 season under the MLV name with 8 teams (Dallas Pulse champion); San Diego Mojo is now listed as defunct and MLV expands to 12 teams for 2027. Pay structure (from the league's 2024 release for 2025): 14-player rosters all at $60,000+, base salaries up to $175,000, ~$10,000 benefits, "total compensation exceeds $1.3 million per team." MLV has a salary cap and luxury tax (cap amount undisclosed) and on Oct 5, 2026 added a Marquee Player Exception. Media (CBS Sports Network, Roku, Scripps/ION, Vice TV) terms are undisclosed.

### Cited Findings
- Merger: PVF to merge with Major League Volleyball, branded MLV, opening play January 2026; PVF raised $40M as part of the transaction valuing the combined leagues at more than $325M — [Yahoo/Sportico](https://sports.yahoo.com/article/pro-volleyball-merger-values-combined-171112737.html); [SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/mlv-pvf-us-volleyball-merger-leagues-august-2025/); [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/other-sports/2025/pro-volleyball-merger-values-combined-leagues-at-325m-1234865847/) (paywalled)
