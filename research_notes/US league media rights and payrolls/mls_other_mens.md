# MLS and other U.S. men's pro team leagues beyond the Big Four: media rights, total payroll, salary-cap structure (as of October 21, 2026)

Scope note: NFL, NBA, MLB and NHL are excluded (covered elsewhere). All figures are labeled by season/year, with "reported" (named outlet) vs. "estimated" (my arithmetic, shown) distinguished. Where a league publishes no total payroll, that is stated explicitly and a proxy is computed.

## Key Question 1 — MLS media rights: Apple deal AAV, 2025-26 restructuring, linear partners (Fox / TelevisaUnivision), and next deal

### Takeaway
Apple's 10-year, ~$2.5B MLS deal (headline AAV ~$250M) was restructured in November 2025: the standalone MLS Season Pass paywall was scrapped (all games included in a standard Apple TV subscription from 2026), the deal now ends after the 2028-29 season instead of 2032, Apple waived its post-2027 opt-out, and (per Sportico, anonymous sources) payments were re-set to $200M for 2026, $107.5M for the 2027 "sprint" season, and $275M each for 2027-28 and 2028-29. The Fox and TelevisaUnivision linear deals (2023-2026) expire after this season and no renewal had been reported as of October 2026; MLS is reportedly targeting $400M-$500M per year for its next deal starting 2029-30.

### Cited Findings
**Original deal**
- The original Apple deal, struck in 2022 and effective 2023, is a 10-year agreement worth $2.5 billion; MLS described the November 2025 change as a "major recalibration" and the deal has been "largely shrouded in secrecy." Garber: "It's the beginning, not the end." — [Front Office Sports, Nov 13, 2025](https://frontofficesports.com/mls-folding-its-season-pass-shifting-all-games-to-apple-tv-in-2026/)
- Apple "pays MLS $250 million per year" (the commonly cited AAV). — [Yahoo Sports / Awful Announcing, Jul 26, 2026](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/mls-eyes-400-million-tv-160004334.html); also cited by [Awful Announcing](https://awfulannouncing.com/mls/apple-scrap-season-pass-games-available-all-subscribers.html)

**Season Pass scrapped (effective 2026)**
- Starting with the 2026 season, all MLS matches and exclusive programming are included in a standard Apple TV subscription at no additional cost; Season Pass previously cost $14.99/month, $99/season, or $79 for Apple TV subscribers. Standard Apple TV costs $12.99/month or $99/year. MLS Board of Governors approved the change. Season Pass per-match viewership averaged ~120,000 and had grown about half during 2025; Apple audience figures are not measured through a standard audited process. — [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/mls-folding-its-season-pass-shifting-all-games-to-apple-tv-in-2026/); [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/46954801/mls-apple-tv-scrapping-season-pass-26-season-sources); [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-uz/111113)
- MLS cited the experiment of making 200+ regular-season matches (incl. all Sunday Night Soccer games) available to all Apple TV subscribers in 2025, which increased viewership, and alignment with Apple's other live sports (e.g., Formula One) that carry no extra paywall. — [Awful Announcing](https://awfulannouncing.com/mls/apple-scrap-season-pass-games-available-all-subscribers.html)

**Revised financial terms (reported, not official)**
- Sportico (Nov 2025, anonymous sources): MLS receives **$200M for the 2026 season**, **$107.5M for the shortened 2027 "sprint"**, and **$275M each for 2027-28 and 2028-29**; the new terms are a roughly **$50M increase** over what MLS would have been paid through June 2029 under the prior deal; the partnership now ends 3.5 years earlier than the original decade-long term; Apple waived its contractual right to terminate after 2027. Garber called the changes "very positive for us." — [Sportico, "Apple TV MLS Streaming Deal Will End in 2029 Under Revised Terms"](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/soccer/2025/apple-mls-streaming-contract-change-1234876902/) (Sportico page itself is paywalled/redirecting; figures corroborated via [Sportcal](https://www.sportcal.com/media/apple-and-mls-to-end-streaming-deal-three-years-early/), [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/11/14/mls-apple-tv-deal-ends-in-2029-years-ahead-of-schedule), [Goal.com](https://www.goal.com/en/lists/mls-and-apple-reportedly-revise-streaming-deal-higher-payments-early-2029-end-date-and-opt-out-removed-ahead-of-2026-world-cup/bltfd56e66b1724572b), and [Sports Media Watch](https://www.sportsmediawatch.com/?p=1335808))
- The deal was originally to run through 2033 (i.e., 2032 season per other sources) and now ends after the 2028-29 season; MLS teams do not expect to regain local rights after the Apple deal. — [Awful Announcing](https://awfulannouncing.com/mls/teams-do-not-expect-regain-local-rights-after-apple-deal.html)
- Sportico separately analyzed whether the end of Season Pass dims MLS's revenue-share hopes (the original deal included subscription revenue-share above a threshold). — [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/soccer/2025/mls-season-pass-apple-tv-subscribers-price-revenue-change-1234876763/)

**Linear partners**
- Dec 2022: MLS announced four-year (2023-2026) U.S. linear agreements with FOX Sports (34 regular-season games/yr incl. 15 on FOX, eight Audi MLS Cup Playoff matches, and MLS Cup every year on FOX/FS1/FOX Deportes) and TelevisaUnivision (21 Leagues Cup matches per tournament incl. the final on Univision/UNIMÁS/TUDN), plus TSN/RDS in Canada; the deals were designed to end in 2026, the World Cup year. MLS deputy commissioner Gary Stevenson said Apple endorsed selected linear agreements. Financial terms were not disclosed. — [MLSsoccer.com](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-on-linear-tv-fox-sports-televisaunivision-tsn-rds-reach-multi-year-deals); [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/business/media/2022/mls-fox-tv-broadcast-apple-1234698348/); [SportBusiness](https://www.sportbusiness.com/news/mls-strikes-four-year-linear-deal-with-fox-sports-as-espn-run-comes-to-end/)
- The Fox renewal ended ESPN/ABC's 26-year run as an MLS partner. — [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/convergent-tv/mls-and-fox-sports-strike-4-year-renewal-disney-deal-expires/)

**Next deal / leadership**
- ESPN's Ben Strauss reported MLS is seeking **$400M-$500M per year** in its next media deal when the Apple agreement expires after the 2029 season. — [Yahoo Sports / Awful Announcing, Jul 26, 2026](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/mls-eyes-400-million-tv-160004334.html)
- LAFC co-managing owner Larry Berg was voted by the Board of Governors (2:1 supermajority, over former Fox executive David Nathanson) to succeed Don Garber as the third MLS commissioner; Garber steps down at the end of calendar 2026 and Berg takes over Jan. 1 (2027) and must sell his LAFC stake. — [SI](https://www.si.com/soccer/major-league-soccer-timetable-commissioner-larry-berg); [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/soccer/mls-hires-search-firm-to-run-hunt-for-new-commissioner-with-don-garbers-term-set-to-end-in-2027)

### Inferences
- Combined U.S. media AAV (estimate): Apple's revised schedule averages roughly $214M/yr across 2026-2029 (($200M + $107.5M + $275M + $275M) / 4 = $214.4M), but on a full-season basis the 2027-28 and 2028-29 payments ($275M) exceed the original ~$250M headline AAV. Fox and TelevisaUnivision fees are undisclosed, so no sourced "all-in" AAV exists; the commonly reported figure for MLS's total media AAV is Apple's ~$250M.
- With the Fox/Univision deals lapsing after 2026 and the Apple exclusivity now ending in 2029, MLS will negotiate both a short-term linear package (2027-) and a full new cycle (2029-30 on) under a new commissioner.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve the Sportico article text directly (redirect to tollbit paywall, DNS failure); figures are as relayed by Sportico via search summary and corroborated by secondary outlets citing Sportico.
- No reporting found (as of Oct 21, 2026) on a Fox or TelevisaUnivision renewal/replacement for 2027+, nor on any Apple sublicense of linear rights.
- Fox/Univision rights fees were never disclosed; no sourced combined AAV.

## Key Question 3 — MLS 2026 salary cap structure (Salary Budget, max charge, GAM/TAM, DPs, U22), soft cap, and 2027 calendar

### Takeaway
MLS runs a **soft cap**: a $6,425,000 2026 Salary Budget covering only the 20-man Senior Roster, a $803,125 maximum budget charge, with Designated Players (up to 3) charged only $803,125 regardless of salary, GAM ($3.28M base per club, up to +$2M under the U22 model) and TAM ($2.125M per club in 2026) used to buy down charges, and 11 off-budget Supplemental slots. The Board of Governors approved a move to a summer-to-spring calendar beginning July 2027, bridged by a 14-game "sprint" season Feb-May 2027.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 Salary Budget **$6,425,000**; Maximum Salary Budget Charge **$803,125**; Senior Minimum $113,400; Reserve Minimum $88,025; active roster up to 30 (Senior Roster slots 1-20 count against budget; slots 19-20 optional; minimum charge imputed for unfilled slots below 18; Supplemental slots 21-31 off budget). — [MLSsoccer.com Roster Rules and Regulations (2026)](https://www.mlssoccer.com/about/roster-rules-and-regulations)
- GAM: base **$3,280,000** per club; up to **$2,000,000** additional under the U22 Initiative Model; cap of $3,000,000/season on GAM from transfers, loans and cash-for-player trades; GAM can reduce a charge to the lesser of 50% of the charge or $150,000 (not applicable when offsetting loan/transfer fees). — [MLSsoccer.com](https://www.mlssoccer.com/about/roster-rules-and-regulations)
- TAM: **$2,125,000 per club in 2026** ($2,025,000 in 2027); eligible player must earn more than $803,125 and up to a $1,803,125 ceiling; charge cannot be bought below $150,000; TAM cannot be traded. — [MLSsoccer.com](https://www.mlssoccer.com/about/roster-rules-and-regulations)
- Designated Players: up to 3 under Three-DP model or 2 under U22 Initiative model; DP (age 24+) budget charge $803,125; mid-season DP charge $401,562.50; Young DP charge $150,000 (20 or younger) / $200,000 (21-23); third DP slot costs a $150,000 fee to the league annually (waived for Young DP). U22 Initiative: 3-4 slots; charge $150,000 (20 or younger) / $200,000 (21-25); compensation generally capped at $803,125. Clubs may switch models between July 1 and Sept 2, 2026. Roster Freeze Oct 9, 2026. — [MLSsoccer.com](https://www.mlssoccer.com/about/roster-rules-and-regulations)
- Calendar: Board of Governors formally approved the shift at November 2025 Palm Beach meetings; regular season to start mid/late July and end with playoffs/MLS Cup in May, with a midwinter break mid-December to early February; 2026 runs as normal; a 14-game "sprint" regular season runs February-May 2027; the first full summer-to-spring season kicks off mid/late July 2027 and ends late May 2028. — [MLSsoccer.com](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-to-align-calendar-with-top-leagues-around-world); [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/mls-votes-to-align-with-global-soccer-calendar-beginning-in-2027/); [CBS Sports](https://mauth.cbssports.com/soccer/news/major-league-soccer-to-adopt-summer-to-spring-schedule-starting-in-2027/)

### Inferences
- Cap x teams proxy (estimate): $6,425,000 x 30 clubs = **$192.75M** of "on-budget" spend, i.e., only ~31% of the $630.96M actually paid in guaranteed compensation. Adding base GAM ($3.28M x 30 = $98.4M) and TAM ($2.125M x 30 = $63.75M) gives ~$354.9M of league-allocated spending capacity, still well below actual payroll because DP salaries above the max charge and Supplemental-roster salaries are paid by clubs outside the budget.
- The structure is a soft cap in practice: no hard ceiling on total payroll, only on budget charges; the binding constraints are slot counts (DP/U22) and the $803,125 max charge.

### Gaps
- Some third-party explainers show stale 2026 figures (e.g., $5.47M budget / $683,750 max charge); the official MLS page figures above should be treated as authoritative.
- No official statement found on how the 2027 "sprint" season affects the salary budget or MLSPA guide timing.

## Key Question 4 — USL Championship and USL League One: media, salary structure, payroll, USL Premier (Division One)

### Takeaway
USL's U.S. rights sit with CBS Sports (multi-year deal through 2027, ~100 matches/yr across CBS, CBS Sports Network, Golazo Network and Paramount+) alongside ESPN networks/ESPN+ and TUDN; no fee disclosed. There is no salary cap; a new five-year CBA (ratified Sept 4, 2026) sets a $42,000 Championship minimum (housing included) for 2026, and a $67,500 minimum for USL Premier, the Division One league targeted for 2028. No total payroll is published.

### Cited Findings
- CBS Sports multi-year rights agreement covers USL Championship and League One through the 2027 season, ~100 matches a year across CBS networks and Paramount+. — [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/soccer/news/cbs-sports-reaches-multi-year-rights-agreement-to-air-usl-championship-league-one-matches-through-2027-season); [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/usl-cbs-agree-media-deal-through-2027/)
- 2026 USL Championship: 25 clubs (13 East, 12 West; expansion Brooklyn FC and Sporting Jacksonville); 34-week regular season ending Oct 24, 2026; games air nationally on ESPN Networks, CBS Sports Network, CBS Sports Golazo Network, ESPN+ and TUDN; the 2026 Final airs on the CBS Television Network Nov 20-22; described as the third year of a four-year CBS deal. — [USL Championship 2026 season structure](https://www.oursportscentral.com/services/releases/usl-championship-confirms-2026-season-structure/n-6307745); [USL Championship](https://www.uslchampionship.com/news_article/show/1351433)
- CBA ratified Sept 4, 2026: five years through the 2030 season; USL Championship minimum base compensation **$42,000 in 2026** (includes value of housing/stipend) with scheduled annual increases; USL Premier minimum begins at **$67,500**; every club must offer health insurance at or above ACA Silver level and health costs no longer count toward minimum compensation; separate entry-level contract structure; 12-month contracts. — [USLSoccer.com](https://www.uslsoccer.com/news_article/show/1366196); [Beyond the 90](https://beyondthe90.substack.com/p/usl-championship-and-premier-cba)
- Entry-level: up to four U-23 slots at a reduced $34,000 minimum (reported as starting 2027). Prior CBA (2021) minimum was $2,600/month for 2025 (other outlets: $3,100/month over 10 months incl. housing/insurance). — [Goal.com](https://www.goal.com/en-us/lists/usl-uslpa-reach-verbal-agreement-on-landmark-cba-after-lengthy-negotiation-period/blt08770650d772a230); [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/48107960/usl-championship-opener-uncertain-amid-ongoing-cba-dispute)
- USL Premier (Division One): announced Jan 2026, targeted 2028 start, single national table, long-term target of 20 clubs, promotion/relegation with Championship and League One; no inaugural clubs named yet; U.S. Soccer D1 sanctioning (min. 12 teams, broadcast partner, etc.) not yet confirmed. ESPN earlier reported USL's proposal of D1 minimums of $55,000 (2028), $60,000 (2029), $65,000 (2030), $70,000 (2031). — [Inside World Football](https://www.insideworldfootball.com/2026/01/28/usl-premier-will-name-tier-one-mls-challenger-launching-2028/); [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/47259172/new-top-tier-usl-proposes-55k-minimum-salary); [CBS Sports](https://www.golf.cbssports.com/soccer/news/usl-to-launch-first-division-mens-league-heres-everything-to-know-about-potential-mls-rival/)

### Inferences
- No salary cap exists in USL Championship or League One; team budgets are club-determined with a CBA floor. Floor-based proxy (estimate, low confidence): if each of 25 Championship clubs carried ~25 players at the $42,000 minimum, the floor payroll would be 25 x 25 x $42,000 = **$26.25M**; actual spend is higher (many clubs reportedly spend several times the minimum), but no club-level or league-level payroll is published.

### Gaps
- No published total payroll for USL Championship or League One; no team-budget disclosures; CBS/ESPN rights fees not disclosed.
- League One 2026 team count and separate CBA terms not researched in detail (League One is not covered by the Championship/Premier CBA per the release wording).

## Key Question 5 — UFL (spring football): media, player pay/CBA, payroll estimate

### Takeaway
The UFL is co-owned by its broadcasters' parents (FOX and ESPN among the investors) so there is no arm's-length rights fee; every game airs on FOX/FS1 or ABC/ESPN/ESPN2 (plus NFL Network), streaming on ESPN app, Fox One and DAZN. The 2025-26 CBA set an active-player minimum of $64,000 in 2026 ($6,400/week, housing stipend folded in) across eight teams; the CBA expires after 2026. Estimated league payroll: roughly $22M.

### Cited Findings
- CBA ratified April 18, 2025, two years (2025 and 2026 seasons); annual salary for active players $55,000 to $62,005 in 2025 and **$64,000 in 2026**; weekly $5,500 to $6,205 (2025) and **$6,400 (2026)**, figures include the reallocated $400/week housing stipend; active roster rises from 42 to 43; training-camp invitees 58 to 64; healthcare four months in-season + three months offseason + five months subsidized COBRA; bonuses $500 Player of the Week, $5,000 Player of the Year, $7,500 MVP, $5,000 per player on championship team. — [First Alert 4 / KMOV](https://www.firstalert4.com/2025/04/18/ufl-players-secure-salary-boost-healthcare-new-collective-bargaining-agreement-2025-2026/); [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/united-football-league/story/_/id/45007604/ufl-ratifies-new-cba-deal-runs-2026-season); [Front Office Sports on X](https://x.com/FOS/status/1913288386913202219)
- Players under contract but off the active roster have earned $2,500/week; training-camp pay $850/week; top quarterbacks reportedly earn ~$100,000-$250,000 on individual deals (unconfirmed by league). — [UFL News Hub](https://uflnewshub.com/columns/ufl-player-salaries)
- 2026 season: eight teams (Birmingham, Columbus Aviators, Dallas, DC, Houston, Louisville Kings, Orlando Storm, St. Louis); every game on FOX/FS1, ABC/ESPN/ESPN2 or NFL Network; streaming via ESPN app, Fox One, DAZN; Louisville Kings won the 2026 United Bowl. — [Wikipedia 2026 UFL season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_UFL_season); [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/ufl/everything-know-about-2026-ufl-season)
- 2026 attendance averaged 10,501 (down ~20% from 2025); Fox game ratings up 4% to 670,000 average; ESPN/NFL Network up 8% to 686,000. — [NBC Sports / PFT](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill/news/attendance-down-ratings-up-for-ufl-in-third-season); [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/ufl-sees-modest-tv-gains-173038339.html)
- Ownership/investors: Mike Repole and Impact Capital, RedBird Capital Partners, FOX, Dany Garcia, Dwayne Johnson, ESPN. — [theUFL.com About](https://www.theufl.com/about); Impact Capital bought a large minority stake July 2025 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_UFL_season)

### Inferences
- Media rights AAV: none reported; because FOX and ESPN are equity owners, there is no disclosed rights fee (estimate: effectively $0 external fee / in-kind distribution).
- Total payroll proxy (estimate): $64,000 x 43 active-roster players x 8 teams = **$22.0M** for 2026, before off-roster ($2,500/wk) and camp pay, QB premiums and bonuses. If rosters were 50 the figure would be $25.6M. No official league payroll is published.

### Gaps
- No reported rights-fee value for Fox/ESPN; no public league financials.
- CBA expires after 2026; no reporting found on a 2027 CBA or 2027 salary scale as of Oct 2026. 2027 season team list (eight teams incl. UFL Colorado, Rhode Island Breakers) comes from a league page of uncertain currency.

## Key Question 6 — Premier Lacrosse League (PLL): ESPN deal, salary pool/cap

### Takeaway
ESPN renewed with the PLL for five years starting 2026 (value undisclosed) and took a reported ~3% equity stake. The PLL does not publish its current cap; the last public figure is a $735,000 per-team annual pool (2021-22, 98% must be spent, $25,000 minimum), and the league announced an 11% cap increase and a 15% rise in average player compensation for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- June 25, 2025: ESPN's renewed deal is five years beginning with the 2026 season, covering all PLL regular-season, All-Star, playoff and championship games plus Women's Lacrosse League games; ESPN takes a minority equity stake (~3% per a person familiar); rights fee not disclosed; PLL growth since 2019: paid tickets +34%, attendance +13%, ticket revenue +149%, sponsor dollars +100%+. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/25/espn-premier-lacrosse-league-media-deal-equity-stake.html); [NBC Boston](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/business/money-report/espn-renews-media-deal-with-professional-lacrosse-takes-an-equity-stake-in-the-league/3751215/)
- Dec 11, 2025: for 2026, "average player compensation is increasing 15% year over year from 2025" and "the salary cap is being increased by 11%"; playoff bonuses and a championship bonus added; merch royalty % and stock options increased; season opens May 9 with championship weekend Sept 20. — [Sticks In Lacrosse](https://www.sticksinlacrosse.com/post/pll-to-open-2026-on-may-9th-with-expanded-summer-schedule-player-compensation-set-to-increase)
- Historical cap: $735,000 annual pool per team, 98% must be spent on players, $25,000 minimum (2021-22). — [UB Law Sports Forum, Dec 2021](https://ublawsportsforum.com/2021/12/15/premier-lacrosse-league-takes-shape/); [Sticks In Lacrosse free agency primer](https://www.sticksinlacrosse.com/post/premier-lacrosse-league-free-agency-primer)
- Eight PLL teams (Boston, California, Carolina, Denver, Maryland, New York, Philadelphia, Utah). — [Wikipedia 2026 PLL season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Premier_Lacrosse_League_season)
- Typical base pay reported at $25,000-$35,000 per season (blog estimate, not official). — [Maryland Sports Blog](https://marylandsportsblog.com/how-much-do-pro-lacrosse-players-get-paid-breaking-down-salaries-from-superstars-to-role-players/)

### Inferences
- Payroll proxy (estimate, low confidence): if the per-team pool had remained $735,000 through 2025, an 11% increase implies ~$816,000 per team in 2026; x 8 teams = **~$6.5M** league-wide.
