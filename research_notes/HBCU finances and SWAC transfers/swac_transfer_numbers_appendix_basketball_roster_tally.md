# Appendix: SWAC basketball year-over-year roster tally (researcher's own count from ESPN data)

Data appendix to `swac_transfer_numbers.md`. Everything here is the researcher's OWN TALLY, not a published count. Pulled November 27, 2026.

## Method

- Source: ESPN's public JSON endpoints, pulled November 27, 2026.
  - Team season player statistics (who played for each SWAC team in a season, games and total minutes): `https://site.web.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/basketball/{mens|womens}-college-basketball/teams/{teamId}/athletes/statistics?season={year}` (example: https://site.web.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/basketball/mens-college-basketball/teams/2296/athletes/statistics?season=2026)
  - Player career statistics with the team for each season: `https://site.web.api.espn.com/apis/common/v3/sports/basketball/{mens|womens}-college-basketball/athletes/{athleteId}/stats` (example: https://site.web.api.espn.com/apis/common/v3/sports/basketball/mens-college-basketball/athletes/5175049/stats)
  - Current 2026-27 roster: `https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/basketball/{mens|womens}-college-basketball/teams/{teamId}/roster`
  - ESPN team IDs used: Alabama A&M 2010, Alabama State 2011, Alcorn State 2016, Arkansas-Pine Bluff 2029, Bethune-Cookman 2065, Florida A&M 50, Grambling State 2755, Jackson State 2296, Mississippi Valley State 2400, Prairie View A&M 2504, Southern 2582, Texas Southern 2640.
- "Played in season" = appeared in at least one game for that SWAC team that season according to ESPN. Players who never appeared in a game (redshirts, injured, walk-ons with no minutes) are not counted at all.
- "Returned" = appears in the same team's statistics the next season, or (for 2026-27 only) is on ESPN's current roster for that team.
- "Played for another ESPN-tracked team next season" = the player's ESPN career file shows games for a different team the following season. This is an ACTUAL-TRANSFER measure, not a portal-entry measure. ESPN tracks all Division I teams plus non-Division I teams only in games against Division I opponents, so moves to Division II, NAIA or junior college are only partly captured.
- "No appearance anywhere next season" mixes players who exhausted eligibility, graduated, turned professional, quit, moved to a level ESPN does not track, or transferred but did not play (injury or redshirt). ESPN's data does not separate these, and it does not give scholarship or walk-on status or graduate-transfer status.
- "First-year player" = no ESPN game appearance for that SWAC team in any earlier season. "Arrived with prior D-I seasons elsewhere" = ESPN shows earlier seasons at a different team (junior college and most non-D-I seasons are invisible, so this undercounts incoming transfers).
- "Departing transfers on 3rd+ D-I school" = players who played elsewhere the next season and whose ESPN career file then shows three or more different teams.
- Destination tiers: power conference = ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, SEC, Big East (only Alabama, Mississippi State, Providence, Seton Hall and Texas Tech appear). Non-D-I = Albany State, Arkansas Baptist, Central State, Georgetown (KY), Georgia College, Kentucky Wesleyan, Lane, Malone, Menlo, Mid America Christian, Midwestern State, Slippery Rock, Southern Arkansas, Tuskegee, UC Merced, West Virginia State. One destination, "Saint Francis Red Wolves" (Cassandra Hawthorne), could not be classified and is counted under "other D-I".
- % of minutes = share of the team's total player minutes in the earlier season.
- The 2025-26 -> 2026-27 tables are a snapshot five to eight games into the 2026-27 season. "Played elsewhere" will rise and "no appearance" will fall as the season goes on.
- Known data blemishes: one Arkansas-Pine Bluff player (Travis Pourciau) appears under two ESPN IDs in 2025-26. Amarr Knox (Alabama State, All-SWAC 2024-25) is listed by ESPN with Eastern Kentucky but has no 2025-26 game appearances in his career file, so he is in "no appearance". Cases like his mean the "played elsewhere" column is a floor for transfers out.
- Check against a published metric: for 2022-23 -> 2023-24 the same method gives returning-minutes shares within about 7 points of Bart Torvik's published figure for 10 of 12 SWAC teams (largest gaps: Alabama A&M 52.2% own vs 36.4% Torvik; Mississippi Valley State 43.4% vs 59.0%).
