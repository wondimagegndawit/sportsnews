## The one item that needs a decision: Lindenwood University

The audited statements are for the parent, Lindenwood Education System, not for the university alone.

- Consolidated system debt: $23,066,667. This is bonds of $23,223,328 less $156,661 of unamortized issuance costs, shown on the balance sheet as "Note payable".
- Debt in the Lindenwood University, LLC column of the consolidating schedule: $0. The whole $23,066,667 is in the Dorsey column (the Michigan career-school subsidiary). The parent-only column is empty too.
- Who the document says issued the bonds (note 17): "In December 2023, the System issued tax-exempt bonds (the Bonds) through the Michigan Finance Authority totaling $24,163,000." and "The Bonds are secured by a pledge of the Sytem's investments." The document never uses the words obligor, borrower or guarantor.
- All $161,125,617 of long-term investments, the collateral, are in the university's column.
- A year earlier the predecessor $24,000,000 bank note and a $7,960,000 line of credit were in the university's column.

I entered $0 because the brief's entity rule asks for the institution's own figure when a consolidating schedule separates it. The first researcher's $23,066,667 is correct to the dollar as the consolidated system figure. Whichever is shown needs a footnote giving the other.

Other points on this filing:
- The line of credit ($30,000,000 facility) had no balance at June 30, 2024.
- All leases are operating leases; the university column's share is $3,825,450.
- RubinBrown did not audit the two Michigan subsidiaries; another firm did.
- The auditor's report is dated October 4, 2024, but the single-audit reports bound behind it say the report on the statements was dated March 13, 2025.
- Net assets fell $18.0 million in the year and $38 million was drawn from the endowment.

## Davidson College: $92,250,000 confirmed

- Senior Note Series A $25,000,000 and Series B $67,250,000, both 2.46%, due March 1, 2050. These are included (current portion $1,685,000, noncurrent $90,565,000).
- Taxable commercial paper $12,650,000, "maturing no later than 270 days after the date of issuance of each note". It sits in the same "Notes payable" caption but in current liabilities ($14,335,000 current = $1,685,000 + $12,650,000). Excluded under the rule.
- The document is not fully consistent with itself: the repayment table in note 7 puts the commercial paper in "Subsequent years" (after 2029), not in 2025, which implies the College expects to keep rolling it over. All notes payable together are $104,900,000.
- KPMG, unmodified opinion, November 26, 2024. No leases or bank lines on the balance sheet.

## Gardner-Webb University: $17,587,600 confirmed

- 2018 Educational Facilities Revenue Bonds: principal $17,635,000, plus unamortized premium $250,225, less unamortized loan fees $297,625.
- Accrued interest of $440,875 is inside the balance-sheet line "Long-term debt and accrued interest payable" ($18,028,475) and has been taken out.
- No leases, notes or lines of credit anywhere in the statements.
- Cherry Bekaert, unmodified opinion, October 25, 2024. Management believes it met its bond covenants.

## Liberty University: $8,255,982 confirmed

- The only obligations that meet the rule are finance leases: $432,995 current plus $7,822,987 long-term. Mostly communications-tower leases.
- No bonds, notes, loans or credit lines. All bonds were redeemed on April 5, 2023, in the prior fiscal year. No interest was paid in FY2024.
- Operating leases of $8,380,280 are excluded. Eleven standby letters of credit ($1,506,848) are off-balance-sheet and undrawn.
- The figure is consolidated with subsidiaries. A supplementary schedule shows the university alone with total lease liabilities of only $2,852,328, so most of the finance leases belong to subsidiaries; the split is not disclosed.
- BDO, unmodified opinion, November 14, 2024.

## Saint Mary's College of California: $107,009,000 confirmed

- Statements are in thousands. Bonds payable carrying value $106,364 thousand = $110,000 principal less $1,473 issuance costs less $2,163 discount. All noncurrent.
- Finance lease (a standby generator) $645 thousand, included.
- Line of credit $4,000 thousand: a current liability on its own line, "available through March 31, 2025". Excluded. Operating leases $190 thousand excluded.
- The notes disclose going-concern conditions and conclude that management's plan alleviates the doubt. The College depends on the remaining $16 million of bond proceeds and on extra endowment draws. Baker Tilly's opinion is unmodified with no going-concern paragraph, dated November 26, 2024.
- Debt more than doubled in the year when the $110 million 2023A bonds were issued.

## Central Connecticut State University: held at system level, confirmed

- The combining schedule in the system report shows "-" for bonds payable and note payable in the CCSU column. All of it is in the System Office column: bonds $267,018,227 and an installment note $18,834,224, with no allocation to any campus.
- CCSU's own lease liability is $370,925 ($156,255 current plus $214,670 noncurrent). These are right-to-use equipment leases under GASB 87, so they are memo only, not debt. The first researcher had them in the finance-lease component.
- CCSU subscription liabilities of $1,632,349 are also memo only.
- State general obligation bonds for academic buildings are not on the system's books at all.
- No separate audited CCSU statements exist; the Federal Audit Clearinghouse has no 2024 submission for the campus or the system.
- Grant Thornton, unmodified opinion with two emphasis paragraphs unrelated to debt, February 25, 2025.

## Northwestern State University of Louisiana: zero confirmed

- In the system report's combining schedule the Northwestern State column has no amount on the bonds payable, notes payable, lease obligations or subscription obligations lines, current or noncurrent.
- The blank cells are true zeros: the amounts that are shown add exactly to the column's totals ($14,479,007 current and $156,335,502 noncurrent).
- The last bond issue ($430,000 outstanding at the start of the year) was paid off during FY2024.
- Student housing is privately financed under public-private partnerships; the university records no liability for it.
- "NSU Facilities Corporation" in this report belongs to Nicholls State, not Northwestern State.
- Louisiana Legislative Auditor, unmodified opinion dated December 30, 2024; the emphasis paragraph concerns the University of New Orleans.
