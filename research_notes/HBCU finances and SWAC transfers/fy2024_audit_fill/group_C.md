## Shorter College (UNITID 107840)

Source: FAC report 2024-06-GSAFAC-0000363930, single audit for June 30, 2024, Ramsey Eddington CPA, report dated March 28, 2025.

- **Debt, $2,526,594.** Balance sheet (p.5): current portion of long-term debt $60,501 and long-term debt net of current portion $2,466,093. The note (p.13) lists two notes payable: U.S. Department of Education at 1%, $126,730 remaining, and an Eagle Bank note originated September 13, 2023, $2,399,864. The two sum to the balance-sheet total. No bonds, no finance leases, no line of credit. Credit cards ($17,409) excluded.
- The Eagle Bank note is new FY2024 borrowing. IPEDS shows $0, which is a reporting gap.
- **Endowment, $600,500.** Balance sheet caption "Restricted Cash - Endowment" (p.5), equal to net assets with donor restrictions. The endowment note (p.11) says the fund was set up by the college in FY2022 and "the current balance is $600,000", with $2,719 of interest; the note and the balance sheet differ by $500. The whole amount is cash in a bank account, not invested.
- 990 cross-check (not audited): FY2024 Form 990 liabilities and assets equal the audit; unsecured notes $2,466,093 equals the noncurrent portion.

## Items needing a human decision

1. **GASB 87 lease obligations (Grambling $2,813,563; Coahoma $14,168).** Both schools have no bonds or notes, and both are recorded as $0, matching IPEDS. GASB does not split leases into finance and operating, so these right-to-use leases were excluded as operating-type leases. If the workbook counts GASB 87 lease liabilities as debt, the values become $2,813,563 and $14,168. One rule should be applied across all public schools.
2. **Arkansas Baptist debt.** The $2,267,426 is a repayment obligation to the Department of Education from audit findings, which the auditor labels a long-term loan. Decide whether that counts as debt.
3. **Alabama A&M endowment.** No audited document labels a figure. Either request the Foundation and Trust audits, or decide whether the Trust's net position ($50,802,742 at July 31, 2024) may be shown as a labelled proxy.
4. **Arkansas Baptist endowment.** Not disclosed; cannot be filled from the audit.
5. **Shorter endowment.** $600,500 on the balance sheet against $600,000 in the note.
6. **Simmons endowment.** $63,455 comes from the donor-restriction note, not the endowment note.
7. **Southwestern Christian endowment.** Whether to add the $40,341 beneficial interest in a perpetual trust.
8. **Denmark Tech endowment.** Foundation holdings are unquantified.
