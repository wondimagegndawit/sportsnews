## Decision needed: Paine College line of credit

- Balance 7,465,293 at June 30, 2024 and 2023, fully drawn, no repayments in either year.
- Balance sheet (PDF p.6): unclassified; the line has its own caption, "Bank line of credit", separate from "Notes payable".
- Note 12 (p.25): "The line of credit has no maturity date." It is secured mainly by permanently endowed funds. Nothing in the balance sheet or notes 11-12 calls it long-term or due on demand.
- Under the rule's wording for exactly this case (unclassified balance sheet, no maturity beyond one year stated) it is **excluded**, giving 9,472,065.
- Against that: the College's Department of Education disclosures count it as long-term debt. Note 18 (p.32-33, inside the audited notes) shows "Total Debt at June 30, 2024: 16,937,358", and the supplementary schedule (p.35) shows "Long-term debt - for long-term purposes - Total 16,937,358". That is 9,472,065 + 7,465,293.
- Why I did not treat that caption as decisive: the same audit firm's identical schedule for Livingstone puts a line of credit that Livingstone's balance sheet classifies as current, and that matured in February 2025, under the same "Long-term debt" caption. The caption appears to be filled with all borrowings regardless of term.
- If the coordinator prefers to accept the caption, Paine becomes 16,937,358.

## School by school

### Hampton University: 130,545,954 (consolidated)
- KPMG, unmodified opinion dated December 23, 2024. Consolidated statements only.
- Bonds payable, net 101,120,368 plus notes payable 29,425,586. No finance leases, no line of credit.
- Hotel subsidiary (27th Street Hotel, LLC, 58.7% owned, acquired June 2024): 24,500,000 of the notes payable as carried on the balance sheet. The note 9 narrative gives 24,472,952 (21,972,952 senior nonrecourse note plus 2,500,000 junior unsecured note); the 27,048 gap is one monthly principal instalment.
- University excluding the hotel: 106,045,954. This is derived by subtraction. The statements have no consolidating schedule and no University-only column, so they do not present both figures.
- Caveats: 13,230,623 of further federal HBCU Capital Financing advances after year end; a material weakness over investment reconciliations; investments fell from 366.4 million to 244.9 million.

### Bennett College: 66,481
- DMJPS, unmodified opinion dated October 17, 2024.
- The 66,481 is an office-equipment financing lease that expires in 2025. No bonds or notes.
- Line of credit 6,472,770 with the investment manager: unclassified balance sheet, own caption "Line of credit", note 9 says it "has no maturity date". Excluded under the rule.
- The Department of Education schedule lists it on the template line "Line of credit for long-term purposes" but reports "Long-term debt - for long-term purposes" as nil, so the College itself did not put it in the long-term-debt caption.
- With the line counted the total would be 6,539,251. The rule value understates what the College owes a lender.
