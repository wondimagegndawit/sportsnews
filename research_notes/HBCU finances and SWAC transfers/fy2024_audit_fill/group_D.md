## Alabama community colleges (general)

The brief's context suggested the Alabama Examiners of Public Accounts. In practice the FY2024 audits of these three colleges were done by private CPA firms and are posted by the Alabama Community College System (accs.edu) and the colleges. examiners.alabama.gov now redirects to the Legislature's site (alison.legislature.state.al.us/epa-home); I did not find FY2024 Examiners reports for these three colleges there and did not need them.

All three affiliated foundations have a December 31 year end, so the foundation year that falls inside the FY2024 window is calendar 2023.

## H Councill Trenholm State Community College (102313): endowment

- Read: "Annual Financial Report for the Fiscal Year Ended September 30, 2024 - Restated", Forvis Mazars, reissued January 12, 2026 (original January 17, 2025). https://www.accs.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/TREN-FY24-RESTATED.pdf
- College-held endowment: none. Statement of net position (p.28-29) has no endowment line and no restricted net position at all. The only investment is "Long-Term Investments 1,193,267", which Note 2 (p.43) says is a non-negotiable certificate of deposit.
- Foundation: the college's statements do not mention the Trenholm State Foundation, Inc. (EIN 68-0525744). I found no audited foundation statements for 2023 or 2024; the foundation page on trenholmstate.edu lists audits only through 2014.
- Cross-check only: the foundation's 2023 Form 990-EZ shows total assets $221,200 at 12/31/2023, including "Title III Endowment CD balance" $101,189.
- Also read: the original FY2024 report (same investment figure) and the FY2025 report (no foundation or endowment information).
- Restatement: the reissue adds about $3.2 million of Pell receivable and revenue (Note 12, p.60). It does not affect this item.

## J. F. Drake State Community and Technical College (101462): endowment

- Read: "Financial Statements, September 30, 2024", Mauldin & Jenkins, LLC, January 14, 2025. https://www.accs.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Drake-FY24-Audit.pdf
- College-held: "Endowment Investments 209,743" (p.13); "Endowment Income 32,486" (p.15).
- Foundation: not in the FY2024 report. The FY2025 report (https://www.accs.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/DRAKE-FY25.pdf, Mauldin & Jenkins, January 15, 2026) presents Drake State Technical College Foundation, Inc. for the first time, as of December 31, 2024. Its endowment roll-forward (p.68) opens with "Endowment net assets at December 31, 2023 $38,334" and closes at $271,734.
- Total: 209,743 + 38,334 = 248,077.
- Weak points: the foundation figure is a prior-year opening balance taken from the FY2025 report; the two dates are nine months apart; the foundation's own 2023 Form 990 says it held no endowment and was not audited; the college's net position line "Restricted - Nonexpendable Scholarships" is blank even though the asset is captioned as endowment.
- Cross-check only: foundation Form 990 net assets $384,250 at 12/31/2023, versus $478,508 opening net assets in the FY2025 audited report.

## Shelton State Community College (102067): endowment

- Read: "Annual Financial Report for the Fiscal Year Ended September 30, 2024", Forvis Mazars, January 21, 2025. https://www.sheltonstate.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/FY24-Audited-Financial-Statements-and-Single-Audit.pdf (same report at accs.edu).
- College-held endowment: none. Investments of $11,946,150 are certificates of deposit (p.46); restricted net position is expendable only (p.32).
- Foundation: Note 11 (p.63) says "This report contains no financial statements of Shelton State Community College Foundation, Inc." The foundation's 2023 Form 990 states its statements were not audited. I found no foundation financial statements on the college site.
- Cross-check only: foundation 2023 Form 990, Schedule D Part V, endowment end-of-year balance $2,578,775 at 12/31/2023; net assets $3,903,960.
- Also read: the FY2025 college report, which repeats the same exclusion.

## St Philip's College (227854): endowment

- Read: Alamo Community College District ACFR for the years ended August 31, 2024 and 2023, CliftonLarsonAllen LLP, December 18, 2024. Linked from https://www.alamo.edu/about-us/compliance/financial-information/ ("Fiscal Year 2024").
- The District holds no endowment (net position on p.44 has expendable restrictions only).
- All endowment is in the district-wide Alamo Colleges Foundation, Inc., presented as of December 31, 2023 (p.45, p.91-102): endowed funds $24,966,744, accumulated gains $10,127,562, non-endowed restricted funds $43,432,716, total net assets $78,948,889; 222 endowment funds.
- Nothing in the report divides any of this among the five colleges. I did not allocate.
- Not obtained: the foundation's stand-alone audited statements (available on request from the District). A foundation fund list returned a 404 error. The FY2024 Annual Investment Report has no endowment or St Philip's content.
- Searched: alamo.edu financial information page; web searches for St Philip's endowment, Title III endowment, and the foundation's audited statements.

## Southern University Law Center (440916) and Southern University at New Orleans (160630): debt

- Read: "Southern University and A&M College System Annual Financial Report for the Period Ended June 30, 2024", Louisiana Legislative Auditor, report dated December 15, 2025, issued December 17, 2025 (Report ID 80240092). https://app.lla.state.la.us/publicreports.nsf/0/ff59d2e8e84a231d86258d63006ccacf/$file/000095aaa.pdf
- Schedule 4, Combining Schedule of Net Position by Campus (p.80-81): the Law Center and New Orleans columns have no notes payable, no finance purchase obligations and no lease liability. In both columns compensated absences, net pension liability and OPEB add up exactly to total noncurrent liabilities, and capital assets equal net investment in capital assets.
- System debt of $19,854,617 (HBCU Capital Financing advance $989,617 to the Baton Rouge campus; finance purchase obligations $18,865,000) sits in the A&M College and Shreveport columns.
- Schedule 4 is supplementary information covered by an "in relation to" opinion.
- The audit was issued about 17.5 months after year end; beginning net position was restated.
- An LLA search hit labelled FY2024 turned out to be the FY2023 report (issued June 2025); I did not use it.

## Bluefield State University (237215): debt

- Read: "Financial Statements and Required Supplementary Information, Years Ended June 30, 2024 and 2023", CliftonLarsonAllen LLP, October 10, 2024. https://wvhepc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/BSU-FS-FINAL-10.11.24.pdf
- No bonds, notes, loans or leases on the statement of net position (p.15) or in Note 8 (p.41). The only contractual liability is the subscription liability of $313,859 (excluded).
- Note 10 (p.49-50): system bonds are the Commission's obligation; the institution's estimated payable is $-0-; assessment paid $10,846. The note prints the dates "June 30, 2023 and 2022", apparently stale text.

## University of the District of Columbia (131399): debt

- Read: "Basic Financial Statements for the Fiscal Years Ended September 30, 2024 and 2023", McConnell Jones, LLP, January 3, 2025, transmitted as OIG No. 24-1-23GG (January 31, 2025), from oig.dc.gov.
- No bonds or notes payable (p.20). MD&A: "The University does not have any debt related to its capital assets" (p.11) and "the University has no separate long-term borrowing authority" (p.19).
- Lease liabilities $27,057,518 (7,652,223 current + 19,405,295 long-term), GASB 87 right-to-use leases of buildings, equipment and a ground lease (p.52). Excluded as operating-type; see decision below.
- Capital projects are funded by District capital appropriations ($35,667,956 in FY2024).
- Also read: the District of Columbia FY2024 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report (cfo.dc.gov). Every mention of the University was checked; none allocates District debt to it.
