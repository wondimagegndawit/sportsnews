## Davidson College (UNITID 198385)
- Source: The Trustees of Davidson College, Uniform Guidance Reports, June 30, 2024, KPMG LLP, report dated November 26, 2024. FAC report 2024-06-GSAFAC-0000355426: https://app.fac.gov/dissemination/report/pdf/2024-06-GSAFAC-0000355426
- Balance sheet (PDF p.5): Notes payable $14,335,000 current and $90,565,000 noncurrent, total $104,900,000.
- Note 7 (PDF p.19): Senior Note Series A $25,000,000 and Series B $67,250,000 (2.46%, due 2050), plus $12,650,000 of taxable commercial paper maturing within 270 days.
- Entered: long-term $92,250,000 (the two senior notes); commercial paper $12,650,000 shown separately as short-term. If all notes payable are wanted, the figure is $104,900,000.
- No bonds, finance leases or lines of credit. IPEDS $0 is a reporting gap.
- 990 cross-check agrees with the split ($92,250,000 and $12,650,000).

## Gardner-Webb University (UNITID 198561)
- Source: Financial Statements and Accompanying Information, years ended June 30, 2024 and 2023, Cherry Bekaert LLP, report dated October 25, 2024. FAC report 2024-06-GSAFAC-0000344084: https://app.fac.gov/dissemination/report/pdf/2024-06-GSAFAC-0000344084
- Balance sheet (PDF p.6): one line, "Long-term debt and accrued interest payable" $18,028,475.
- Note 8 (PDF p.22): 2018 Educational Facilities Revenue Bonds $17,635,000, unamortized premium $250,225, accrued interest $440,875, unamortized loan fees ($297,625).
- Entered: $17,587,600 = 17,635,000 + 250,225 - 297,625 (carrying value without accrued interest). Alternatives: principal only $17,635,000; balance-sheet line with accrued interest $18,028,475.
- No notes, leases or line of credit in the statements. Covenant compliance asserted by management. IPEDS $0 is a reporting gap.

## Liberty University (UNITID 232557)
- Source: Liberty University, Inc., Consolidated Financial Statements and Supplementary Information, years ended June 30, 2024 and 2023, BDO USA, P.C., report dated November 14, 2024. FAC report 2024-06-GSAFAC-0000065941: https://app.fac.gov/dissemination/report/pdf/2024-06-GSAFAC-0000065941
- Balance sheet (PDF p.8) has no bonds, notes or debt line. Note 10 (PDF pp.27-28): Series 2012 ($100,000,000) and Series 2019 ($89,750,000) taxable bonds were fully redeemed on April 5, 2023 (FY2023). No interest expense in FY2024.
- Note 11 (PDF pp.29-30): finance lease liabilities $432,995 current + $7,822,987 long-term = $8,255,982; operating lease liabilities $8,380,280 (excluded).
- Entered: $8,255,982, all finance leases. Liberty has no borrowed-money debt, so IPEDS $0 is essentially right; showing $0 or $8,255,982 is a presentation choice.

## Lindenwood University (UNITID 177968)
- Source: Lindenwood Education System, Consolidated Financial Statements, June 30, 2024, RubinBrown LLP, report dated October 4, 2024 (Dorsey subsidiaries audited by other auditors). FAC report 2024-06-GSAFAC-0000360701: https://app.fac.gov/dissemination/report/pdf/2024-06-GSAFAC-0000360701
- Balance sheet (PDF p.7): "Note payable" $23,066,667; lines of credit nil; lease liabilities $21,019,622 (all operating; "The System does not have any finance leases", PDF p.24).
- Note 17 (PDF pp.42-43): the amount is tax-exempt bonds issued December 2023 through the Michigan Finance Authority ($24,163,000 issued; $23,223,328 outstanding less $156,661 issuance costs = $23,066,667), which retired a $24,000,000 bank note.
- Entity problem: the consolidating statement of financial position (PDF p.48) puts the whole $23,066,667 in the Dorsey column and nothing in the Lindenwood University, LLC column. The bonds are secured by a pledge of the System's investments, which sit in the university column.
- Entered: $23,066,667 (consolidated audited figure), confidence medium. If the workbook wants the university entity alone, the figure is $0.
- 990 cross-check for EIN 43-0652649 (Lindenwood Education System) shows no bonds or notes and total liabilities of $20,979,818, consistent with the debt sitting in Dorsey College Holdings.

## Saint Mary's College of California (UNITID 123554)
- Source: Financial Statements and Federal Awards, June 30, 2024, Baker Tilly US, LLP, report dated November 26, 2024 (statements in thousands). FAC report 2024-06-GSAFAC-0000354946: https://app.fac.gov/dissemination/report/pdf/2024-06-GSAFAC-0000354946
- Balance sheet (PDF p.5): long-term debt, net $106,364 thousand; line of credit $4,000 thousand; leases $612 + $223 thousand.
- Note 8 (PDF p.22): CEFA 2023A Revenue Bonds $110,000 thousand, less issuance costs $1,473 and discount $2,163 = $106,364 thousand. Note 9 (PDF p.24): finance lease liability $645 thousand; operating $190 thousand (excluded).
- Entered: $107,009,000 = 106,364,000 + 645,000; line of credit $4,000,000 separate.
- Careful-reader items: debt rose from $50,241 thousand to $106,364 thousand in FY2024 (November 2023 refinancing that also paid off the credit line and the swap). The notes include a going-concern discussion; management concludes its plan alleviates the substantial doubt. IPEDS $0 is a reporting gap.

## Central Connecticut State University (UNITID 128771)
- Source: Connecticut State Universities, Annual Comprehensive Financial Report for the year ended June 30, 2024, Grant Thornton LLP, report dated February 25, 2025: https://ccsu.edu/sites/default/files/2025-03/CSUS-Financial-Statements-FY24.pdf
- There is no stand-alone audited CCSU statement. CCSU is a column in the supplemental combining schedule of net position (PDF p.60), which the auditor reports is fairly stated in relation to the basic statements.
- In that schedule all bonds payable ($21,370,000 current + $245,648,227 noncurrent = $267,018,227) and the installment note ($3,139,036 + $15,695,188 = $18,834,224) are in the System Office column. The CCSU column shows no bonds and no note; it shows leases payable of $156,255 + $214,670 = $370,925.
- Note 1 (PDF p.29) says the System Office centrally administers debt service and the allocation of bond proceeds to the universities. Note 14 (PDF p.49) says the CHEFA bonds are issued directly by CSUS, and that State general obligation bonds for academic facilities are not CSUS debt.
- Entered: held_at_system_level, value blank, with CCSU's own lease liability ($370,925) recorded as a component. No allocation of system debt was made.
- Not found: any official campus allocation of the CHEFA bonds. The "FY24 Annual Report" on ccsu.edu is the CCSU Foundation donor report and has no debt data.

## Northwestern State University of Louisiana (UNITID 160038)
- Source: University of Louisiana System, Financial Statement Audit for the Year Ended June 30, 2024, Louisiana Legislative Auditor, report dated December 30, 2024, issued December 31, 2024: https://lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/ceaac10dcadb296d86258c030070835f/$file/0000895c.pdf
- There is no stand-alone audited NSU statement. Schedule 4, Combining Schedule of Net Position by University (PDF pp.138 and 140), shows nothing in the Northwestern State column for bonds payable, notes payable, lease obligations or subscription obligations.
- Note 12 (PDF p.89): NSU's only issue, Wellness, Recreation, and Activity Center Bonds Series 2011, had $430,000 outstanding at July 1, 2023, all redeemed in FY2024. PDF p.97: remaining pledge is $0 at June 30, 2024.
- Entered: confirmed_zero. The IPEDS $0 is a true zero as of June 30, 2024.
- Context: student housing is financed by private operators under public-private partnerships (PDF pp.117-118) and is not a university liability in the report.
- Not found: a stand-alone NSU Annual Fiscal Report for FY2024 on nsula.edu.

## What was searched
- Federal Audit Clearinghouse API (api.fac.gov) by EIN for all seven; the five private schools have audit-year 2024 submissions, downloaded from app.fac.gov. The two public campuses have no FAC submission under the EINs given.
- Web searches for the Connecticut State Universities FY2024 financial statements and for the University of Louisiana System and Northwestern State FY2024 audits on lla.la.gov; a search of nsula.edu for an FY2024 Annual Fiscal Report.
- ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API for the five private EINs (cross-check only).
- EMMA was not consulted; the audited statements were sufficient for every school.
