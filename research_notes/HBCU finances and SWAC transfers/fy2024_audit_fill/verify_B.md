## School by school

**Saint Augustine's University — corrected to 15,817,964.** The balance sheet has one debt line, "Long-term debt 15,817,964", and no current/noncurrent split. Note 8 shows it is two lines of credit (11,504,098 and 4,175,000) plus 138,866 of vehicle loans. Because the audited statements carry both lines under their own long-term debt caption, the rule includes them. The auditor's report carries a going-concern paragraph. The 4,175,000 line is scheduled to come due within a year, and the note's maturity table does not add up as printed.

**Shaw University — confirmed at 6,309,969.** This is the 2023 promissory note maturing July 2028. The 4,933,000 securities-based line is on its own line, has no stated maturity and sits under no long-term caption, so it is excluded. It is the same kind of instrument that is included for Saint Augustine's; the difference is only in how each balance sheet captions it. Counting it would give 11,242,969. An audit finding says the former CFO drew on the line without the President's or Board's knowledge.

**Dillard University — confirmed at 8,330,744.** HBCU Capital Financing loan 7,981,172 plus finance leases 349,572. The loan is carried gross; its 882,988 of issuance costs sit as a separate asset, so there is no net-versus-gross difference. The 3,900,000 drawn on a bank line is captioned "Note payable" on the balance sheet, has no stated maturity and is excluded; including it would give 12,230,744.
The finance-lease question is settled in favour of the balance sheet. The note table that totals 262,841 is the prior year's table with its first row removed; it was not updated for leases added in FY2024. The balance sheet, the financial responsibility schedule and the 2024 column of the FY2025 audit all show 349,572.
The loan was not new in FY2024: 883,628 was outstanding at 30 June 2023 and the agreement dates from October 2022. It stood at 36,328,833 a year later.

**Stillman College — confirmed at 0, decision needed.** The College owes 4,927,468 on three lines of credit. The balance sheet puts all of it under current liabilities and shows no noncurrent debt, and no maturity is stated, so the rule excludes it. Against that, the note is headed "Long-Term Debt", the cash-flow statement calls the draws "Proceeds from long-term agreements", and the financial responsibility schedule labels the amount long-term debt. If the note heading is taken as the document's classification, the figure is 4,927,468.

**Tougaloo College — corrected to 1,828,109, decision needed.** Notes payable 720,189 and finance leases 20,410 are confirmed. Added: the 1,087,510 line of credit, whose stated maturity (26 August 2025) is more than a year after year end; the rule includes a line on that ground. The balance sheet nonetheless presents it as current, so if presentation is preferred the figure goes back to 740,599. The other two lines (3,342,182 together) are due within a year and excluded. The statements are consolidated with the College's television station company and two other subsidiaries.

**Voorhees University — confirmed zero.** The four liability lines add to total liabilities with no borrowing, lease or line of credit in either year, and the cash-flow statement shows no borrowing or repayment.

**Jarvis Christian University — confirmed at 0.** The only borrowing is a 1,918,035 mortgage-secured line from a church extension board. It is a current liability and was due by 1 January 2025, so it is excluded. The note is headed "Long-Term Debt" but itself shows the whole balance as the current portion. Counting it would give 1,918,035.

**Le Moyne-Owen College — corrected to 5,124,617.** The balance sheet presents the bank line of credit under "Long-term Liabilities" and the note says it "is classified as long-term". The rule therefore includes it. The facility's stated maturity was November 2021 and it is rolled a year at a time; it is secured mainly by endowment assets.

**Morehouse College — confirmed at 3,428,978.** HBCU Capital Financing bonds 3,141,497 net (4,129,306 gross less 987,809 issuance costs) plus finance leases 287,481. The margin facility and the new revolving loan both had zero balances. Only a small part of the 70 million authorised had been drawn.

**Tuskegee University — confirmed at 2,980,207.** HBCU Capital Financing note, net (3,831,096 gross less 850,889 issuance costs). The bank line of credit had a zero balance. Only a small part of the 35 million available had been drawn.

**Florida Memorial University — still not found.** FAC has nothing after audit year 2023 under the EIN, the UEI or the name, and no audit-year-2025 filing whose comparative column could supply the 2024 figures. The Florida Auditor General's index lists other private universities but not this one, and the University's site posts no financial statements. A FY2024 single audit was due 31 March 2025.

## Documents read

Downloaded from FAC and read by me: the FY2024 audits of Saint Augustine's, Shaw, Dillard, Stillman, Tougaloo, Voorhees, Jarvis Christian, Le Moyne-Owen, Morehouse and Tuskegee. In each I read the auditor's report, the balance sheet, the cash-flow statement, the debt, line-of-credit and lease notes and the financial responsibility schedule, and searched the rest of the text for borrowing terms; I did not read every page of every package. Also opened as cross-checks: Dillard FY2023 and FY2025 audits, Tougaloo FY2023 and FY2025 audits. Dillard is a scan; its balance sheet, debt note, fair-value note and lease table were read from page images. Nothing could not be opened.
