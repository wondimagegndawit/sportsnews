## Corrections

1. **J. F. Drake State, endowment: $248,077 becomes $209,743.** The first figure added the college's $209,743 (FY2024 audit) to a foundation balance of $38,334. That $38,334 is the opening line of a roll-forward in the FY2025 report, dated December 31, 2023. The final rule allows the two to be added only when both come from audited FY2024 documents, so only the college figure counts.
2. **Southwestern Christian College, endowment: $267,181 becomes not found.** The statements never caption an endowment figure. The $267,181 is the line "Perpetual in nature" inside net assets with donor restrictions, and the final rule says not to use net assets as a stand-in. The number itself is right, and the note's wording treats it as endowed money held in cash, so this is a labelling decision for you (see below).

## Decisions for the coordinator

- **Southwestern Christian:** leave blank, or show $267,181 labelled as perpetually restricted net assets held in cash.
- **Simmons:** I kept $63,455 because the audit captions that line "Endowment", but it sits in the donor-restricted net assets note. The endowment note itself states only "Board-designated endowment funds $0". The alternative is to show $0 or blank.
- **Arkansas Baptist debt:** the $2,267,426 is a repayment obligation to the Department of Education from audit findings. The auditor labels it a long-term loan, so the rule includes it; you may want a footnote.

## School by school

### Shorter College (107840)
Source: FAC 2024-06-GSAFAC-0000363930, Ramsey Eddington CPA, report dated March 28, 2025, unmodified.

- **Debt $2,526,594, confirmed.** Balance sheet (p.5): "Current Portion of Long-Term Debt 60,501" plus "Long-Term Debt (Net of Current Portion) 2,466,093". The note (p.13) lists two notes that sum to the same total:
  - "Note Payable to Department of Education at 1% annual interest, 60 monthly payments, origination date July 1, 2021": $126,730.
  - "Eagle Bank Note Payable - Interest Only for 24 months, 25 Year Amortization", originated September 13, 2023: $2,399,864.
- No bonds, no finance leases (rentals are month to month), no line of credit. Credit cards of $17,409 are excluded.
- **Endowment $600,500, confirmed.** Two figures appear in the same audit:
  - Balance sheet (p.5): "Restricted Cash - Endowment 600,500".
  - Endowment note (p.11): "The current balance is $600,000."
- I kept $600,500: it is the dated balance-sheet amount, it equals net assets with donor restrictions (600,000 opening plus 500), and the FY2025 audit carries 600,500 forward as its opening balance.
- Caveats: the money is cash in a bank account ("None of the funds in the endowment have been invested"); the note says the college, not donors, established the fund; the cash flow statement shows borrowing proceeds of $1,926,125 against a stated loan of $2,399,864 without explanation.

### Arkansas Baptist College (106306)
Source: FAC 2024-06-GSAFAC-0000426870, Sean M. Bruno CPAs, report dated August 19, 2026 (26 months after year end), unmodified.

- **Debt $2,267,426, confirmed.** How the auditor words it:
  - Balance sheet (p.6): "Due to the U.S. Department of Education (NOTES 5 and 11) 2,267,426".
  - Note 8 (p.23): "DEBT: LONG-TERM LOAN PAYABLE: ABC has a loan payable to the U.S. Department of Education with a term of seven (7) years with a 1% interest rate. The loan resulted from final determinations of Audit Findings with questioned costs from audit years 2013 through 2017."
  - Note 11 (p.24): "ABC's repayment obligations to DOE totaled $2,267,426 at June 30, 2024".
  - The cash flow statement treats the reduction as an operating item.
- **Line of credit $550,000, excluded.** Caption "Line of credit (NOTES 5 and 8)". The balance sheet is unclassified and no note gives a lender, rate, maturity or renewal terms. Recorded in the memo field.
- **Endowment: not stated.** The word does not appear in the statements or notes. Nearest lines are investments $1,453,138 and net assets with donor restrictions $317,919; neither is an endowment figure.
- Caveats: prior period adjustment of $(1,888,532); composite score 0.6; material weaknesses; deferred revenue is $987,229 on the balance sheet but $676,976 in Note 13; cash of $29,366 at year end.
