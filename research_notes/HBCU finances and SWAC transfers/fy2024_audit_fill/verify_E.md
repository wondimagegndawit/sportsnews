## Missouri State University: endowment at June 30, 2024

**Figure to use: $126,270,574.** It is the Foundation's endowment of $120,070,812 plus the University-owned endowment of $6,199,762. The two do not overlap.

Documents read (both Forvis Mazars, LLP, unmodified opinions dated December 5, 2024):
- University: https://www.missouristate.edu/FinancialServices/_Files/FY24MSUUnivReportRevised0325.pdf (69 PDF pages)
- Foundation: https://www.missouristate.edu/FinancialServices/_Files/MSU_Foundation_2024_Audit_Report.pdf (25 PDF pages)

Page numbers below are PDF pages.

(a) **Foundation endowment as stated in its endowment note.** Foundation report, Note 5 "Endowment", p. 14, table "The composition of net assets by type of endowment fund at June 30, 2024":
- "Donor-restricted endowment funds" $116,814,419
- "Board-designated endowment funds" $3,256,393
- "Total endowment funds" $120,070,812 (of which without donor restrictions 3,256,393; purpose or time-restricted 28,617,995; perpetual in nature 88,196,424)

The same table is reproduced in the University report, p. 54.

(b) **University-owned endowment.** University report, Statements of Net Position, p. 14, noncurrent assets, University 2024 column: "Due from Foundation 6,199,762". On p. 15 the same amount is the University's net position "Restricted for Nonexpendable: Scholarships and fellowships 6,199,762". Note 15, p. 48, explains it: an endowment fund of $199,762 transferred to the Foundation "for management purposes" in 1982, plus $6,000,000 of Department of Education endowment grants received in 2023 and transferred the same way. 199,762 + 6,000,000 = 6,199,762.

(c) **Does the Foundation's $120,070,812 already include the University's funds? No.**
- The Foundation records the University's funds as a liability, not as its own net assets. Consolidated Statement of Financial Position, p. 5, under Liabilities: "Funds managed for Missouri State University 6,199,762". The University report shows the same thing from the other side, p. 15, Foundation column: "Due to Missouri State University 6,199,762" under noncurrent liabilities.
- Note 5's $120,070,812 is explicitly a net-asset figure ("composition of net assets by type of endowment fund"; "Endowment net assets, end of year"). A liability cannot be inside net assets.
- The $6,000,000 moved in fiscal 2023 appears in the Foundation's cash flows as a financing item ("Funds transferred from University for investment in endowment 6,000,000", p. 7) and does not appear in the 2023 roll-forward of endowment net assets (University report p. 55: contributions 4,268,974; other additions 776,522; nothing of that size).

Arithmetic: 120,070,812 + 6,199,762 = 126,270,574. The sum is mine; neither report prints it.

**Confidence: high** that the two components are correct as printed and that adding them does not double count. What the documents do not settle:
- The University's $6,199,762 is original principal, not fair value. Both reports say the earnings on those funds are recorded in the Foundation's books, but not in which line, so it is unknown whether those earnings sit inside the $120,070,812. Either way they are counted once at most.
- Neither report states gross endowment investments. The Foundation's endowment investment pool is $121,995,631 at market value (Note 2, p. 11), a different measure that the reports do not reconcile to the endowment net assets.

Difference from the first researcher: the value and both components are unchanged. The caveat that "a small overlap cannot be ruled out" is not supported by the documents and should be dropped; confidence goes from medium to high.

Other points for a careful reader: the audited University covers the West Plains campus as well as Springfield (the unaudited campus schedule on p. 68 puts the whole $6,199,762 under Springfield). The University PDF is a March 2025 re-posting; its "Revision" paragraph (p. 25) covers only an immaterial change to a 2023 liquidity disclosure. No going-concern or emphasis paragraphs.

## UC endowment: double-counting check

**Plain answer.** They are two separate pools owned by two separate legal entities, not one endowment reported twice. Adding a campus's "University endowments" lines to its "Campus foundations' endowments" lines in the annual report table does not count any money twice. The audited notes say so directly. The unaudited campus table has no footnote on the point, so for that table it is an inference from the audited notes.

Documents read for this check:
- UC Annual Financial Report 2023-24 (PwC, November 18, 2024), same file as above.
- UC Annual Endowment Report, fiscal year ended June 30, 2024 (prepared by Mercer Investments, LLC for UC Investments; unaudited market values; 42 PDF pages): https://www.ucop.edu/investment-office/investment-reports/annual-reports/annual-endwoment-report-fy-2023-2024.pdf
- Audited statements, June 30, 2024 and 2023, of three campus foundations, each PwC, September 20, 2024: U.C. San Diego Foundation (https://foundation.ucsd.edu/_files/audited_financials/june2024.pdf), UC Riverside Foundation (https://documents.ucr.edu/foundation/UC_Riverside_Foundation_FYE_2024.pdf), UC Santa Barbara Foundation (https://giving.ucsb.edu/sites/default/files/documents/foundation/UC_Santa_Barbara_Foundation_FY23-24_Financials.pdf).

### 1. Separate legal entities with separate assets: yes

UC annual report, audited notes (PDF pages):
- Note 1, p. 40: "The University has 11 legally separate, tax-exempt, affiliated foundations, one for each campus and the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL). The economic resources received or held by the foundations are entirely for the benefit of the campuses. ... the campus foundations are reported under GASB requirements as discretely presented component units of the University."
- Note 17, p. 100: "Endowments and gifts are held and administered either by the University or by the campus foundations." Page 101 describes the second group as "held by the campus foundations and administered by each of their independent Boards of Trustees". The two groups have separate tables (17.1a and 17.2a) and no combined total.
- Note 21, p. 106: "Although independent boards govern these foundations, their assets are dedicated for the benefit of the University of California."

Foundation audits say the same from the other side:
- UC Riverside Foundation, Note 1, PDF p. 13: "Contributions that are made directly to The Regents for the benefit of UCR are not recorded by the Foundation and are not reflected in the accompanying financial statements; they are reflected in the financial statements of the University."
- UC Santa Barbara Foundation, Note 1, PDF p. 14: "Funds raised through the Foundation on behalf of the Regents are made directly to the Regents and are not included in the accompanying financial statements."
- U.C. San Diego Foundation, MD&A (unaudited), PDF p. 5: "Gifts that are made to UC San Diego through The Regents of the University of California (The Regents) are not included in these statements." Note 1, p. 12, calls the Foundation "a not-for-profit organization formed in 1972" and "a component unit of the University of California".

### 2. Foundation money invested in The Regents' pools: counted in the foundation's column only

Several foundations do invest in the General Endowment Pool (GEP). The audited annual report states how that money is treated:
- Note 3, p. 59: "The campus foundations' cash and cash equivalents and investments that are invested with the University are excluded from the University's statements of net position and included in the campus foundations' statements of net position."
- Note 1, p. 42: "the campus foundations' investments in University-managed investment pools and their allocated share of the securities lending activities have been excluded from the University's financial statements and included in the Campus Foundations' columns."
- Table 3.7a, p. 58, shows the deduction: "Campus foundations' investments with the University (3,673,607)" thousand, of which GEP (3,024,147), taken out before the University's "Total investments $39,914,453".

The foundations carry those holdings on their own balance sheets. San Diego, p. 18: "UC Investments manages 68.5% of total Foundation investments, or $1.15 billion. The Foundation's largest investment in the UC pooled funds is in the General Endowment Pool (GEP) ... reflected above under long-term investments". Riverside, p. 18: "The Foundation holds the majority of investment assets in UC Investments GEP."

The Endowment Report treats it the same way, with the pooled money in columns of its own (section 3.2, PDF p. 7, footnotes):
- "1 Assets managed by Regents in GEP and BGP for the benefit of the campuses excluding investment allocations to the GEP, BGP and STIP by the campus Foundations."
- "2 Foundation endowment assets invested in GEP, BGP, and STIP provided by campus Foundations."
- "3 Assets managed by the campus Foundations excluding investment allocations to the GEP, BGP and STIP."

**Where the documents are silent.** The unaudited "Campus Financial Facts" table says nothing about this. Its only footnotes concern "Other" operating expenses and the Systemwide column. That it follows the audited treatment is supported by one tie: its "Nonspendable endowments" line sums to the audited net-position figure of 1,316,568 thousand, and the University's net position excludes foundation investments.

**Where double counting would arise.** Only by mixing bases. The GEP as a whole does contain foundation money: "Total GEP assets include Regents Investment in GEP, Foundation Investment in GEP, and Other Foundation and Affiliate Investments in GEP" (Endowment Report, PDF p. 6, footnote 1). Adding the whole pool to the foundations' endowments would count that money twice.

### 3. Printed combined totals

- **UC annual report: none.** It prints no combined Regents-plus-foundation endowment figure, per campus or system-wide.
- **UC Annual Endowment Report: yes.** Section 3.2 "Total University Endowed Assets", PDF p. 7, prints "Total Endowment Assets" per campus and system-wide. Each printed total equals the sum of that report's own five columns (three rows differ by 1 thousand from rounding). System-wide: Regents 26,485,499 + Foundation investments in GEP 2,942,813 + in BGP 490 + in STIP 70,862 + Foundation 11,646,338 = 41,146,002 thousand, as printed.
- **The Endowment Report totals do not equal the simple sum of the annual report's two columns.** They are on a different basis: unaudited market values of everything UC Investments manages for the campus in the GEP and the Blue & Gold Pool. The audited annual report (p. 49) describes the Blue & Gold Pool as a vehicle "to invest long-term excess reserves", which is wider than endowment. The Endowment Report says the Blue & Gold Pool was newly included this year (PDF p. 4).

**What this means for the workbook.** If a combined figure is shown, pick one document and stay in it. The annual report table's two groups can be added without overlap. The Endowment Report's printed totals are also free of overlap but are larger and unaudited. Do not take the Regents figure from one document and the foundation figure from the other.
